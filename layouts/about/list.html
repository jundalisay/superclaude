It began with the financial crisis

Superphysics was founded by Juan from his personal research on the 2008 Financial Crisis — a crisis that happened because Alan Greenspan failed to regulate the banking industry, citing Adam Smith as his justification for non-regulation.

So was Adam Smith such a bad guy for causing so much economic pain that actually caused deaths around the world?

Reading The Wealth of Nations told a different tale. Smith advocated strict regulation of banking, comparing it to building firewalls to prevent the spread of fire. Greenspan was really citing Samuelson and neoliberal writers who elevated selfish-interest over the common interest.

Since Greenspan and Samuelson were the bad guys, Smith became the good guy to us:

  • ◆Smith described the invisible hand as a concept of dharma, citing David Hume. We reinterpret his "self-interest" as personal dharma or purpose in life as svadharma, in line with Socrates, Hinduism, and Taoism.
  • ◆Hume agreed with Descartes, and cited Plato.
  • ◆Descartes' aether traces back to Plato.
  • ◆Plato wrote about Socrates.
  • ◆Socrates spoke of reincarnation and chakras, and of dharma versus adharma which was wrongly interpreted as justice or δικία and injustice or ἀδικία.

Socrates became the essential link between Western and Eastern philosophy — the thread we pulled to form Superphysics: a dialectical system for solving problems, the way the scientific method solves science problems and formulas solve math problems.
