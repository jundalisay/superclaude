### The Three Factors of Psychic Functioning

Therefore in this way, there are three basic factors that allow for the functioning of this idea in a clear and concise manner, just as there are certain factors that allow for a clear connection and communication through your telephonic device.


1. Psychic functioning and telepathy will manifest along the line of least resistance.

Now what this means is very simply that if the information that is being requested is accessible without going to another level, then you generally won't go to another level to get it. So if an individual says to you, "What did I have for breakfast?" they already know, so why should the psychic—not that it is not possible for the psychic to do so if that is another circumstance—but why should that ability open up when the answer already exists in the individual asking the question? It is easily accessible through that individual.

And let us say the quickest response, the quickest manifestation of that answer would be simply for the psychic to reply, "Don't you know what you had for breakfast? You tell me."

Therefore you will also understand that another way to express the path of least resistance may be in the following manner: that again the information may be directly accessible on the level in which you exist in the conscious state.

So let us say an individual asks you, "All right, you tell me what time did I get up this morning?" Now you may find that nothing comes to you in your normal psychic manner, but as you are pondering this, you look around and over there for some reason stands out a sign that has the number seven on it. So you simply say, "Well all right, it's a seven." But you recognize that the idea is even so much that you did not have to go to another level to get it so to speak, but it was right there for you and the synchronicity of the event played itself out. So it still took the path of least resistance.


2. There must be a need, very strong need, or a very strong desire, or a very strong trust for the information to be there. 

Without at least one of those factors, there is no need for the activation and no ability for the emotional activation of that information.

If somebody says, "Well what did I have for breakfast this morning?"—who cares? It may be very important to the person who had the breakfast. Generally speaking, however, that type of a question does not usually evoke a great need, desire, or trust, since again it is easily accessible information and generally speaking it is being used to create the idea of a test, which generally as you say turns people off rather than excites them emotionally.
