### Exercise

This is not being done so that you may have more opportunities to judge yourself—you already have enough of those.

Recognize that this is simply being offered to you as a co-creation so that you can see how very creative you are even when you choose to limit yourselves, even when you choose to feel that you do not deserve to know that you are happy, that you do not feel you deserve to have whatever you wish without struggle, without strife.

**Topic: How You Sound to Us**

This for you also may serve to give you some idea—and again we are not meaning to judge you nor to, as you say, call you down—but simply recognize this may give you some sense of how many times when we communicate with you, you sound to us.

For we see you as a total conglomerate of all that you are, and we understand that you are at any given moment of time—as you create those moments to exist—different ideas of yourself.

You become different ideas from moment to moment to moment to moment.

Many times you exemplify this in your speech, and what to you sounds like a coherent statement, to us sounds like three different people talking about three different ways of life.

You think, "Well, what about so-and-so and this relating to such-and-such and that, and then again how about this"—and you think it all is one idea when it is actually three or more.

With every time you state those different ideas, you become a different person—the person that only relates to the idea and exemplifies the idea you are stating at the moment.

When you put the "but" or whatever in between those statements and create for yourself a polarity, a contradiction, a dichotomy, then you become a different person that has now unto yourself saying a specific idea.

Recognize that when you are the person that creates the question and then become the person that knows you have the answer, you may create what you have judged upon your Society up to this point to be a negative habit—you call it absent-mindedness.

But recognize that that is why you usually ascribe—because in your knowingness you know that the idea behind absent-mindedness more often than not is something that puts you in touch with who you are, the center of yourself where you know the answers are.

That is why you usually ascribe the term absent-mindedness to someone you think is smart.

You follow me?

Recognize that you say "absent-minded professor"—geniuses are absent-minded.

They tend to lose track in what you turn to be the practical ideas of day-to-day living, but this is only because they begin to listen to themselves.

Now understand, you would act the same way, and it is not necessarily a negative way to act if you went from a person who separates the answer from yourself and formed a question to a person who listened to the answer or discovered that you had the answer.

It could go something like this:

"Well, Bashar, what about so-and-so and such-and-such?"

Now recognize, as we have said, that as you speak something, you are on the level of that idea or you could not conceive of it.

Therefore, when you say something, when you imagine something, if all of a sudden in mid-sentence you recognized that you became that—if you let yourself recognize that you became what you are conceiving—then you would just be so, and it would be:

"Well, Bashar, what about such and—oh, I see."

"What—what did you have a question? What are you talking about?"

"Talking to me? No, I did not have a question. I know my answer."

"Well, somebody asked a question."

"Well, yes, but that must have been the me I was before. Now I allowed myself to become the me that has the answer, and the me that I am now never asks a question because the me I am now does not need to ask questions to separate the answer from myself."

Therefore, I forgot what I was saying because I am now another me that does not contain that idea.

You follow me?

No.

However, we will generate for your enjoyment—I'll say a condensation, in a way—of many times, and again not to judge you, how you sound to my civilization in all the ways that you have created to hold yourself back from being willing to be who and what you are.

Take it away.
