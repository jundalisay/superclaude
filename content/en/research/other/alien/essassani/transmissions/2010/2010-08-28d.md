## Understanding the Oversoul

This holot hope experience was the first step in the idea of forming a stronger conscious connection to your oversoul. The oversoul is that level of your being, of your consciousness—non-physical in nature—that is a collection of a multitude of individual souls and spirits that express themselves as individual physical beings.

The analogy is similar to the idea of the hand being the oversoul that has many extensions being the fingers that you call lives. These dip down from the oversoul non-physical level through the template level reality, crystallizing into the idea of individuated souls and spirit personalities, and thus then further down, densifying, crystallizing into physical personalities, into individual people, and having experiences in physical reality. But all the physical reality lives go back to the idea of the oversoul.

There are in that sense connections between many of you to the same oversoul vibration, even though an oversoul may experience a multitude of diversified individual experiences as physical beings, as physical people. The idea is that all of them will still be able to key back—their signature vibration, an aspect of their signature vibration, will always be similar to the signature vibration aspect in all the other people, all the other beings that are also part of that oversoul.

That oversoul may choose overall large, multi-generational spanning themes of exploration. An oversoul can not only experience the idea of all the lives simultaneously, but can also not only create what you call past, present, and future lives, but also can split itself into multiple concurrent lives. In other words, many of you already understand the concept you call past lives and future lives, but linearly speaking you consider the idea of each of those lives who have lived and died before the next one begins and ends. But the oversoul can also split itself into multiple concurrent lives living simultaneously with you.

Many of you may meet some of your concurrent incarnations from the same oversoul. Some of you may never meet in the life—it depends upon the theme, it depends upon the reason why such concurrent incarnations were created. Some of you may meet your concurrent incarnations and be friends; some of you may be enemies. It doesn't necessarily mean one thing or another just because it comes from the same oversoul that you do—it depends on the theme of exploration that the oversoul is looking for the experience.

### 300,000 Oversouls for 6 Billion People

You have upon your planet at this time approximately 6 billion people, as you count in your language. But of all those 6 billion people, they are the extension of only about 300,000 oversouls. So the idea is that you are all very intimately intertwined in a variety of ways—not only shall we say laterally through all the web connections represented by the idea of one single plane represented by the geometric pattern of the holot, but also through the oversoul, multi-dimensionally on many different levels.

### The Prime Oversoul

Being that the idea of different dimensions simply is another way of saying different vibrational frequencies, different levels, there are of course oversouls for oversouls, and oversouls of oversouls of oversouls—all the way back to what you call All-That-Is. And of course, all the way back to the one.

Now the one is the undifferentiated oversoul of all. The one does not know itself—there is no differentiation, no reflection, no self-awareness in the one. The one is just the one; it is just existence itself. But within the one, since it is the one and must by definition contain any possibility whatsoever, thus then one aspect of the one is that which is that moment of self-awareness by which the one thus then in that reflection became self-aware and knows itself as All-That-Is.

So the one is the primary oversoul that is undifferentiated and knows not itself. And All-That-Is is the oversoul of all oversouls and knows and perceives that it is the oversoul of all oversouls. You may call it God, goddess, Creator, the infinite—you may call it anything you wish. It doesn't care, because all names are created within All-That-Is. So all names are of and from All-That-Is, and all names are of it—no name is outside of it.

### Deep Sea Diving Analogy

You can look at each and every one of your lives as the oversoul deciding to go deep sea diving. It places upon itself the idea that it requires certain equipment to experience physical reality—in the same way that you require certain equipment to breathe underwater under the ocean. Being freer in the air, like the oversoul is free in non-physical reality, it imposes upon itself the mask, the breather, the tank, and goes diving—deep, deep down into the sea, the deep blue sea of physical reality.

There is always a connection to the surface, but the idea to understand is that when you are in the deep blue sea, the deeper you go, it is a little difficult to see that much farther in front of you. But being on the surface and above the surface, the higher mind and the oversoul together can work to lay out the course to say: "We can see to the horizon and beyond. We will guide you. We will guide you if you will simply follow that light, follow that frequency, follow that sound, follow that knowledge, follow that vibration of being yourself, of your joy, your excitement—then you will not go astray."

And whatever you encounter down there in the deep blue sea, you'll be able to learn from it, to absorb it, to apply it into your life in a positive and constructive and creative way. And one day, when you thus then rise back to the surface, take off your mask, take off the breather, take off your tanks, and free yourself once again and wake up into spirit, you will have a better understanding and a clearer connection to the idea of the higher mind and the oversoul that has always been there guiding you like a beacon.
