## 1992: The Year of the Dream

We may call your year of 1992 the year of the dream. 

The opportunity to truly fall into step, into alignment with your dream, and avail yourself of the momentum of the acceleration that is now taking place upon your planet, to fall into synchronous step with this energy so as in a sense to not be left behind, so to speak.

Because as we have said, the energy on your planet now is changing. The energy of the reality that you prefer is in a sense thickening and crystallizing. 

Those who do not now take action in the direction of their dreams, in the direction of their passion, in the direction of their excitement, especially in this upcoming year of 1992, will find it much more difficult to implement their dreams and desires in the years thereafter.

For the acceleration will be so great that it may take a complete collapse of any other system that you are holding on to that you no longer prefer in order for you to be able to avail yourself of the path that will lead to your dream.

So we suggest that you take advantage of the momentum now while it is still, shall we say, catchable, while you are still able to shift slowly, relatively speaking, into that strength so that you can have a buffer of time that will allow you the opportunity to process the things that you need to process within yourself in order to allow the transition to be smooth for you.

So you do not necessarily have to allow everything around you to come crashing down before you will create the opportunity to see that there is nothing left for you but your dream. Allow yourselves the opportunity to take heart that you can live your dream, but you must take action. You must move in those directions. You must take the step.
