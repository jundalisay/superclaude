For you will understand that this exercise is done with your eyes wide open. This meditation is with your eyes wide open.

Now here is what we wish you to do. Just look around you at the room you are in. Just look. Slowly. Open your eyes very, very, very wide. And when you look at something, really look at it as if for the very first time. Just look. Now here is the part that you will practice and get stronger at day by day. Imagine that while you are a physical consciousness—we are not going to ask you to do something that is not natural to you—but while you are used to seeing from the point of view of your physical eyes, I'm going to ask you now to think about this view that you appear to be having from your physical eyes in a very different way.

Think about it now in a very different way. Pretend. Imagine that as you are looking at everything very closely, that your physical eyes are not where you are looking from, but that your physical eyes are mirrors in a periscope.

Normally, when you look through a periscope, you look into mirrors which are angled in such a way that your vision bounces into the mirror and up into other mirrors and then out to look at the scene you wish to see, grabbing the handles of the periscope and looking around to see the reality that you are peering into.

Your physical body is the periscope. The other end of the periscope. Whatever you see is not being seen by the physical you. Whatever you see is being seen by the higher, non-physical you through the periscopic mirror of the physical body.
