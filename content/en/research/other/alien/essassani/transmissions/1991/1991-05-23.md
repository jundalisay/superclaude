Those that have moved ahead on this path of choosing what excites you in life, that the things that no longer excite you—the things that you used to do in life, the things that you used to hold on to by playing it safe just in case the thing that you really want to do doesn't work out—no longer work for you anymore. ## 1. You exist.

But this also carries profound import for your Society. For understand something about existence: existence has only one quality, only one nature. It exists. 

It does not know how to do anything else. It cannot become anything else. It has never been anything else. Existence has one quality: existence. It has always existed. 

It exists now. 

It always will exist. Because time is subservient to existence; existence is not subservient to time. Time is a creation of the infinite; the infinite is not subject to time. Existence always exists. Therefore, understand the profound implication of this idea: if you exist now, it means you always have and you always will. 

You are Eternal. You are, in that sense, infinite beings, aspects and reflections of All-That-Is, of creation, of the infinite. That which exists cannot become nonexistent. 

Oh yes, it can change. It can transform and become existent in many different ways, all the ways that existence can exist. Yes, you will always change, for change is the only constant. But the idea is that you will never cease to be. You will always understand yourself as a Consciousness. You will always know yourself. You will always be self-aware. For you exist, and therefore that is your state of being. That is your most fundamental quality, your fundamental nature. As we have said many times, there is no need to worry about the idea of that which exists becoming non-existence. Non-existence is its own quality, is its own space as it were. 

There is no room in non-existence for anything new. Non-existence is already full of all the things that will never exist. There is no room for that which exists. One does not become the other. These are completely different ideas. So the first absolute is that you exist. It is your very most fundamental, foundational nature, and it is an eternal one.
