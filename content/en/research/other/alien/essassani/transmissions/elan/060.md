Those who are on a spiritual search, will come across the notion that all is one. 

However, very often, individuals do not entirely understand how completely accurate this is. 

They will express this oneness as them having certain connectedness to all things. 

From the physical framework, this will do. 

However, from an overall perspective, when you speak about connections in general, this implies separate things which are connected, and this is not exactly the idea about all is one.

For understand, all is literally one expression, one event. When you unroll that event according to a particular idea that you call a timeline, it seems in that way to be experienced as a series of separate events. 

But in that way, when you experience reality from the perspective of what you sometimes call your higher self, all of that linear motion, all of that unfoldment, all of those series of events actually comprise one overall event. For everything from the perspective of eternal, infinite, multi-dimensional creation is, in that sense, one thing literally. 

How this will, shall we say, break down in your reality, as you can understand that everything is, in that way, directly related to you, an expression of you, and your particular perceptions of your reality are just that: your individual perceptions of the way that you project your reality. You are not truly even within a reality. More accurately, again, from the perspective of all being one, you are the reality.

When you begin to progress in your expansion of your consciousness, and you, quote unquote, reach the next level, so to speak, the realization that always accompanies that reaching of the next level is that the reality that you previously believed you existed within, exists within you. Now, you even have, in that way, a very concrete example in your physical reality of this idea. When you go to sleep at night, you have another kind of dream. We say another kind of dream because, from our perspective, this is a dream as well, although with a slightly different definition. But when you awaken again into this dream from that dream, even though while in the nighttime sleeping dream reality you felt, while you were in it, that it was real—which it was—when you wake up, you on some level understand that the reality that you had just previously believed to exist within, existed within you. 

This is at least one concrete example of what you experience when you, so to speak, wake up from this dream. You understand, understand that this dream also exists within you, and so on, and so on, and so on, into infinity. Therefore, because everything is a direct representation or extension, if you wish to separate it even that much, of you, you can understand that everything in your reality has a significance to you. All, again as we have sometimes shared, events, all situations, are but a neutral set of props with no built-in meaning. 

You supply that meaning, and according to the meaning that you—listen to the wording—choose to supply, whether that be consciously or unconsciously, that determines the effect that you get out.

When you apply a negative label to something, negative implies, in that way, separative, whereas positive, in that way, implies integrative, which brings everything back together to the all-one thing. When you assign a negative label, you are separating the situation you are labeling from yourself, and this, at least effectively, experientially, puts it out of your reach to change it, for it is keeping separation upon separation. When you decide to choose a new and assign positive meanings to circumstance, events, and relationships, you then are, shall we say, approaching it from an integrative perspective, where you understand everything is all one thing. And the result of labeling a particular situation in a positive way, regardless of its face, being that a positive labeling is integrating, will allow the situation to integrate within you, and the face of it will then transform. What seems to, quote unquote, repetitive negative symbols seeming to repeat, is that negative or separative assignment, which in a sense is not taking responsibility for the integrative reason that you chose to create. Therefore, the realization that all is one allows you to take back your power, even your power to interpret your reality in a positive way. For no matter what, no matter what, anything in your reality, absolutely anything... 

Therefore, when things come around, allow yourself to step back, so to speak. If it is obviously negative, understand that this is one face of the neutral event. When we speak about events being neutral, we are not necessarily suggesting, after you neutralize them—or at least recognize that they are neutral—that you leave them there, for they do not serve you by simply remaining neutral. What we are suggesting is going the next step, and the next step will be to glean how that situation is of service to you. How even that, even that, serves you to allow you to learn more about yourself, and to allow you to choose different choices. Again, when we speak of everything being a choice, what this has the underlying imposition of is that there are other choices, and there always are. When you say "this is it," you are writing the rule book. Therefore, perhaps you will experience it as being the only choice, because you have assigned that meaning, and any meaning you always assign will be the effect that you get out. So perhaps, if you wish, we will not force you to have too much fun. You can begin to look at the things that you would automatically, quickly, immediately label negative, and allow yourself the opportunity to see in any way—allow it to be obvious. You do not need to, as you say, pick your brains to show you how it is of service to you. And again, listen to the wording exactly as it is, not how you wish it was. When you allow it to serve you exactly as it is, it will transform, and how you, quote unquote, wish things were will begin to replace these ideas that keep presenting themselves in this way. Everything truly is one thing. All is one, and one is simultaneously all. This is one, perhaps you can say, of the three absolutes. The first absolute would be: you exist. This transcends all realities, and in consciousness exists, it simply is. The second absolute is: all is one, one is all. And the third experiential absolute is: what you put out is what you get back. So if you will simply understand that because you exist, and everything is all one thing, if you will be willing to put out a positive definition, a positive label, what you will get back will always be a positive effect. No, no matter how much your society tells you that is impossible in a given situation, that is only impossible for the individuals who give it that label. So if you wish, again, begin to view, begin to expand. When things seem to overwhelm you, understand that nothing can truly overwhelm you, for you are the reality you are within. There, literally, you are choosing to overwhelm yourself utilizing an external reflection of who you are: a very elaborate game, if you wish, of hide and seek of consciousness. So understand that you can use it all, because it is all one.


We will, in that sense, reserve the channel's voice and cut, shall we say, this introduction a bit short, though nothing is ever lost, for it is all within the one. Therefore, I ask you now: how may I be of service to you? Shedding at this timing, allow us to once again, and for the first time, thank you all for participating and co-creating these interactions with us this evening of your time, on whatever level it is accessible to your current understandings of your reality. Simply, if you wish, incorporate the idea that all is one; you will begin to understand that you have a different relationship with your entire existence, and you will begin, in that sense, to be able to begin to treat all things as an extension of you. You will then begin to derive advantages from things you formerly labeled only bad; you will begin to convert what you have considered to be your greatest liabilities into your most useful assets. Do not force it; you cannot anyway. Your reality is created through you by your allowance, so lighten up, then you will be enlightened.
