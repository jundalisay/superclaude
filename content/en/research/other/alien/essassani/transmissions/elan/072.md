Your society has within it a notion that many individuals choose to buy into—sometimes consciously, other times unconsciously—but either way, it is the notion that change is a laborious process: that in fact you must create, participate in, find, or attract an elaborate process in order to realize change in your life. Now, needless to say, this works, and in that sense it is a true reality; but it is not the only true reality. For whatever it is that you truly believe sets up within you particular feelings or emotions, particular thoughts. That is the prism that comprises your personality: beliefs, emotions, and thoughts. With that premise—with those beliefs, emotions, and thoughts—you then act, and those actions are the actions that create your life. Therefore, whatever you believe, by extension you act that way; and that, in and of itself, is what midwives the physical reality to conform, in that sense, to your truest beliefs. 

So therefore, if you believe in the need for a process, then you can, in that sense, create that process, go through that process, and perhaps then allow yourself to change. That is one way to change. Though we detect that this takes quite a bit of time, and one of the concerns of members of your society is now beginning to view the idea of changing to a preferred you: they prefer it to happen a bit more quickly. Therefore, allow us to share with you a few simple ideas about becoming, in the moment, the thing that you prefer. 

Allow us first to remind you that you are all perfect creators. In fact, in that sense, you perhaps cannot help but create. You always create; it is, as you say, automatic. Therefore, being a creator is simply the way you are, and it is not something you need to muster up. Perhaps the creations you have experienced have not always been according to preference, and this is what we are discussing: beginning to create your reality according to preference. But when we say you are perfect creators, we mean it. Therefore, even when you create the idea of misery, it is perfect misery.


Therefore, allow us to remind you of the following idea: you can only ever exist in, and only ever experience, the now moment. Anytime you ever perceive that you experience, it is always right here and right now. Perhaps you can say that is the only time and place that there are. Therefore, even when you speak of the past or speak of the future, you are creating that past and future in the now moment—not actually going back to some place or forward, but in that now moment creating the notion of a past or a future, because the now moment is the only moment you actually exist within.

You can always create anything right now. More mechanically speaking, understand that though you perceive your reality to be a continuum—continuous—perhaps you can say that each now moment is a complete event. You create the seemingness of now moments in succession, and this creates the illusion of continuity. But in a more literal, mechanical sense, each now moment is a complete package, a complete statement. When you change something in the now moment, it all changes—until you perhaps recreate a now moment with some old definitions; then you bring back the old definitions into that moment. But because you always do create from the present, from the now moment, that shows you in no uncertain terms that the now moment is always your ultimate point of power.

When individuals do change, or make a decision to change, as you say, if they create a process, what they are doing in that sense is only allowing themselves to validate the change when first fulfilling a particular set of criteria. In other words, they could change in the now moment, but they will not grant themselves that change. They perhaps have been taught that you need to earn, or you need to create, that change in a series of steps—which is one way, one truth, one possibility. However, you can simply make the decision in the now moment to change. Just decide to change in that moment. Decide who it is you wish to be. Use your imagination, which is a tool to see yourself being that you.
