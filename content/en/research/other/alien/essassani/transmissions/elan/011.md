Very often you are creating it in such a way that you give it an apparency that it is controlling you, that you are within it. But the paradox lies within the idea that you are creating that apparency—the apparency that you are not creating it.

You are always:

We mean that literally, not figuratively, not nice philosophy, but literally physically creating your reality always using the idea of 100% of your being.

Even if you are using 90% to make it seem as though you are only using 10%. This is a propensity within your society, and in fact the current trend within your scientific thought is that you only use some small percentage of your brain. 

No, this is not true. You always use 100% of your brain.

Maybe your scientific data, your scientific equipment is not in a sense sensitive enough or does not measure certain particular frequencies to allow one to come up with such a premise, but in fact it is very similar to the divisions you have made within your own personalities. 

You are always a 100 percent being, always able to access 100% of yourself. But within your exploration of using 90% to make it seem as though you are only using 10%, you have come up with very many convenient labels for what you have divided yourself up into compartments: subconscious, unconscious, superconscious, ego, superego, id, etc. However, they are merely that—labels, ideas, things which allow you to give your responsibility for the creation of your life away to the label. "Well, it's only my subconscious mind, I'm not really responsible for what's going on." Yes, you can create it that way; you have done so very masterfully. 

You are perfect creators, even if you choose to create misery—it is perfect misery. And therefore again, we suggest and propose to you this idea that you are 100% being, and merely by choosing to view yourself in that way, you may experience that reality. It is that simple.

While we are on the subject of how simple it is, may I say to you: another idea we have observed within your society is that it is not that simple. Things must be difficult; you must strive, you must stress, you must work, you must pay your dues—no pain, no gain. All right, you can't have it that way. But in fact, the reality that it is that simple has just as much validity. We are not saying that your physical universe, your situational universe, the interconnectedness of all things is not complex, is not rich. However, it need not be complicated to perceive that odd complexities—that is richness. But anything can be that simple with that simple realization. By simply validating that that viewpoint may be possible, you then give yourself equal choice to choose between one reality and another reality. Another propensity that we have seen upon your planet is your tendency toward deciding in many or most situations that it must be this or that—"It's got to be this, can't be that; it's this, it's got to be this, it can't be that; it's this, it is this or that." All right, again, everything is real and we grant validity to all realities. However, one other alternate approach to this idea is that it is this and that. "This and that"—"this or that" takes away your ability to choose one or the other; you must choose this or that. No, if you realize that it is this and that, you have equal ability to choose. Therefore, when asked the question, "What is the ultimate truth?"—the ultimate truth is composed of all truths, all realities. No one ultimate truth, or if it did not include all truths, it would not be the truth. Therefore, realize everything is real, everything has validity—it is this and that.

One of the things that we propose again to you is that by making the idea of slight changes in your approach, slight changes in your perception, you will have a grand sweeping change in your reality. Very often with this or that philosophy, you will choose to see when you have made a change within your existence the idea about it is the same old existence with a little change. We perceive that when you do make but a small change, the entire reality does change. Now you can't say it's this or that—it is same reality with a small little change, or you can say small little change changes the whole reality. Once you equalize them, once you grant them equal validity, you can then choose between them. Realize, if you have a cube all sides are blue, you then paint one side of that cube red. You may choose to say, "Oh, it is just the same old cube with a slight facelift on one side," or you may realize it is an entirely different cube. If you decide "same cube, slight facelift," you act in one particular way. If you choose to believe "different cube altogether," you act a different way. 

So your perception—what you buy into, what you most strongly associate with—determines your attitude that you carry with you when you perform action, it is the action which does affect change and or perpetuate your reality the way it is.

Therefore, while realizing you have made a change, you are a different person. You may then choose, instead of acting like the same person with a small slight different change, to realize you are a different person. And then when a situation comes along that in your past the person that you were would have dealt with in a particular way, now you view yourself as a new person and ask yourself, "How would this new person handle the situation?"

You will handle that situation differently and then experience the idea of a whole new person. It starts out on a cognitive level, but you can bring it all the way down to the idea of this reality and manifest it.

This may seem like the same thing as living your positive life and pushing the negative away, but it is a very different idea. 

Granting validity to all is what allows you to then choose all. By putting everything on equal footing, equal ground, that gives you equal ability to choose between these ideas. Therefore, we never in our society from our approach invalidate anything.
