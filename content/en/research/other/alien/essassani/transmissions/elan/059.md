## Meditation

Close your eyes. Take three deep breaths, and as you do so, understand that your imagination is the conduit through which you create your own reality. Your higher self imagines you into physicalized existence. Therefore, this is quite, shall we say, a useful mechanism. As we proceed with what you may label this visualization, understand that in whatever way, whatever way your imagination sees fit to alter anything that is being said, this will perfectly tailor this technique to you. Therefore, do not feel you are not getting it; do not feel you are interfering with the process. It is simply impossible to do so. No matter what you do, no matter how you perceive this idea, you will be doing it correctly, and there simply is no wrong way. So therefore, again, do not fear that you are not getting it. You are.

Again, three more deep breaths. Allow yourself in whatever way is comfortable for you to envision yourself floating in space—in dark, black, endless space. Allow yourself to feel the sensations that you would feel now that you are floating in space. Allow the limbs of your body to seemingly lighten in weight, lighten in substance and mass. Feel yourself suspended in the center of your own galaxy. As you become aware of what you label your physical body, allow the very fine, pinpoint definition of that body to begin in that sense to expand.

Allow this expansion to occur to the point whereby you are literally a bubble—a round, spherical bubble. Imbue the image of yourself contained within the bubble with a blue-white tinge within the substance of the bubble and the skin of the bubble itself. If you wish, for the purposes of distinction, you may envision the skin to be a slightly more thick, skin-like surface, and the interior of the bubble to be what you might label a gelatinous material. Therefore, now feel yourself suspended within the gelatinous material of the bubble—the electromagnetic fluid of the planet that you are on itself—and begin to feel the borders of what you label your physical body to dissolve into that substance. Slowly, begin to lose the sense of what you label your outer boundary, and feel yourself diffuse and take up residence within the bubble. And in fact, you are becoming this bubble of energy itself. Allow your body to completely dissolve within the substance of the bubble, so that you now simply are that bubble suspended again in space, with again a slightly skin-like shell.

To whatever degree you are comfortable, begin to discern, begin to allow yourself to notice that the skin of your bubble—the skin of your very being—vibrates. Allow yourself to perceive that vibration, and understand that this vibration will differ for each one of you, for this vibration is a representation of your very signature itself—your signature vibration. So therefore, use the next few of your seconds as you create time to begin to feel the vibration on the perimeter of your being, the skin of the bubble of energy that you are.

As you begin to get comfortable with this idea of being a sphere of consciousness, you may now understand that you are not alone. You are in fact surrounded by many other bubbles. As you begin to perceive the awareness of these other bubbles, still pay attention to the vibration on the skin of your bubble. Now, in whatever way you are so attracted—again, there is no right, there is no wrong—simply allow yourself to perceive one of the other bubbles around you, understanding that these bubbles represent the other individuals co-creating this interaction with you at this time.

Honing in on one other bubble in particular, allow yourself to now perceive the vibration of the skin of the other bubble. Simply perceive it. As you do perceive the discrete vibration that you are now labeling to be the vibration of the other individual bubble, now begin to compare that vibration to your vibration, seeing where they are similar but also where they differ, still maintaining your individual vibration and observing the individual vibration of the other bubble. As you notice the differences between your vibration and the vibration of the other chosen individual energy bubble, begin in whatever way is comfortable for you to alter your vibration to match the vibration of the other individual. Allow yourself to copy or mimic—whatever way your imagination is creating there to be a difference in the vibrations—allow your vibration to mimic the vibration of the other energy bubble.

When you have succeeded to the best of your ability in mimicking that vibration, begin to notice what thoughts flow into your consciousness. Begin to notice how those thoughts differ from your usual thoughts. As you are now completely identifying with the vibration of another, you are becoming in a sense on a level the other. Therefore, you will begin to perceive thoughts that differ from your own, and they will be representative in a sense of the style of thoughts of the other. Simply allow them into your consciousness, allow them to present to you what they will, and allow yourself to establish whatever significance those thoughts have to you without invalidating them, no matter what they are. We will pause and allow you to do so.

At this time, allow yourselves to begin again to reestablish the vibration that you are. However, understand that now that you have even temporarily become another vibration, there may be a slight alteration in the vibration that you now become, to correspond with what you have just learned and what you have just shared. Again recognize you are surrounded by many other bubbles, and in fact you may now visualize that all the bubbles representing the individuals present are now beginning to, so to speak, coagulate together. In that sense they are still discrete bubbles, but picture yourself with the other bubbles as beginning to be tightly packed into a spherical area. As you in that sense create a nucleus of energy which is representative of the group that is present, now allow yourself off in the distance to notice a surge of oncoming bubbles—billions of them—and allow them to tightly pack around the nucleus of energy that you have just created with this group.

Visualize yourself now again as a bubble, though you are tightly packed on all sides, touching other bubbles. Each portion of your beingness is in contact with the externalized vibration of some other individual, and again, though still discretely individual, part of a nucleus. As you collect and gather together the six billion other bubbles that represent individuals upon your planet, understand again that you are doing so in a spherical form. So now you have a 360-degree all-around spherical sphere of bubbles. Now picture what you call your planet Earth, which is already conveniently a sphere. Allow that vision of your blue-white emerald Earth to become transparent. View this Earth bubble as being quite large, and in fact large enough to encompass the other billions of bubbles. Picture yourself merging with this bubble, entering within this large bubble, so that the skin of the bubble of your planet Earth will now surround and contain all other bubbles, including yourself.

Begin to allow yourself to perceive the skin of the bubble of the planet itself—the energy bubble which is representative of your Earth—and also notice that it too has a vibration, though perhaps comparatively speaking that vibration is quite slow compared to the vibrations you have perceived earlier—approximately 7.5 vibrations per second. If you wish, if this will assist you, picture a low-pitched rumble as you perceive the skin of the energy bubble of your planet itself. Now understand, though you all are still separate and discrete individual bubbles, you are also simultaneously one. Begin to again discern the differences between your individual vibration on the surface of your energy sphere and that of the energy sphere of your Earth. Again begin to compare and contrast. Allow your vibration to now become temporarily again synchronized to the vibration of the planet itself.

As you allow to the fullest extent that you will yourself to synchronize with the vibration of the external wrapping around all the bubbles, one by one allow yourself to perceive each of the bubbles within the large bubble to pop. Listen to the sounds of the many billions of bubbles popping within the one large bubble, and allow yourself to spread out within the substance of that large sphere. Now that you are no longer so contained, allow yourself to mix your substance with the substances of all the other individuals present upon your planet.

Allow yourself now to find the center of this sphere. Even though all other individuals are scurrying to the center, each individual can simultaneously exist there. So simply find yourself in the center of that bubble and begin to expand to encompass all other energies until you finally expand to the total perimeter of the sphere of the planet itself.

Though in many realities there are particular laws, they are simply agreed-upon ideas for experience. Fundamentally speaking, there are no real laws of creation, though perhaps one—one will be encompassed in what you might label three absolutes. So as you are expanding to fill this energy sphere, understand the three absolutes. Number one: you exist. Period. You simply exist. Number two: all is one, one is all. This may seem as a paradox, but it is so. Everything is all one event—different manifestations within and expressions within that one event—but it is all one thing. And the third absolute, and perhaps you can say law that transcends all dimensions of reality, is: what you put out is what you get back.

As you in that sense now encompass the entire energy sphere and understand that you contain your planet, you contain all other individuals—each one of you also experiencing the same thing—this paradox is one of the indications of the multi-dimensional nature of Infinite Creation itself. Understand what has occurred is that you have first of all aligned and synchronized with your own signature vibration. Then you have aligned and synchronized with the signature vibration of another, demonstrating quite truly that to love someone is to know them, to identify with them is to know them. And lastly, understand that on mass you have also synchronized your energy not only to yourselves, not only to each other, but also to the planet that you have chosen to exist upon. For understand that the planet that you exist upon, as all planetary bodies throughout the multiverse, are also conscious, are also consciousness. Therefore, you can harmoniously synchronize with any and all consciousness. Therefore, you are all truly one.

The gateway through which has been discussed upon your planet as of late can only be in that sense entered by one—but this does not mean one single individual; it means all one thing. The second absolute: all is one, one is all. Therefore, if you will, picture as you picture yourself floating in space off in the distance—in whatever way your imagination creates this idea—picture an oncoming black hole. Slowly allow yourself to understand that although it is a common societal notion that black holes suck in all energy and matter around them, that you are attracting it to you as well. As this idea, this gateway, literally approaches your proximity, allow yourself now again a sphere of blue-white energy to simply pass through it at whatever rate is comfortable.

As you do, picture yourself being sucked down a tunnel. As you look and understand, you now see a light at the end of that tunnel, so to speak. Simply allow yourself to be moved, to attract that light to yourself. As you approach the end of this tunnel, you may again for the purposes of analogy view it to be a slide or a chute. Simply allow yourself to now begin to resume what you consider to be your human form, and feet first, slide down the tunnel. In this way, as you detect that you are approaching the end of the tunnel, allow yourself to shoot out the tunnel and land squarely on your feet. Allow what you consider to be your surroundings to now begin to resolidify around you. Allow the definition of this room to now begin to appear, and at whatever rate again is comfortable for you, open your eyes and begin again to assume what you label and define to be full consciousness.

Allow me the joy to wish you and at the same time entitle this interaction: happy birthday. Understand you all always recreate yourself in every given moment. Most often, this recreation has so many similar definitions to what you had previously created yourself to be that you seem to be a continuous person. However, this is only because of the definitions within and with which you redefine and recreate yourself in every given moment. Continuity between each moment is in that sense a perception; each moment is discrete. You may now understand in a very organized fashion that you have literally recreated yourself again—synchronized to your signature, synchronized to the others that are present, synchronized to all others that you perceive within your reality, and synchronized to the vibration of your planet itself.

Simply understand: what you label discomfort and disease can only exist within a vessel or a body that is misaligned. You are now—at least at this moment—all perfectly aligned, reigned in a sense to each other, to yourselves, to the planet. You may if you wish continue to recreate yourself as a realigned being; it is up to you. We will not force you to have too much fun. But understand in no uncertain terms: you are born anew in each and every moment. However, this has been a bit more of an intentional exchange of energy. It is again no accident that you are here with the individuals that are present with you this evening, and there is just the perfect amount of energy, the perfect amount of individuals, to allow the full positive culmination of what you might call this exercise.

Therefore, good day, good morning—this is a new morning in your new life. I thank you for allowing me to interact with you in this recreation of yourselves, both individually and collectively. I derive great joy from interacting with your society in whatever way that that takes form. There is no expectation on my end that these interactions need go any particular way. I simply understand that however they go will be absolutely perfect for everybody involved, as well as myself and the others from our end that are involved in this interaction. This is the idea of synchronicity, and synchronicity is within the understanding that everything is all one thing. That therefore, everything within that all-one-thing—by definition being part of the same one thing—is connected, is interwoven. Therefore, there is nothing extraneous. There is nothing that does not belong. By definition, if it exists, it exists. This simple understanding can now have you assume the perspective that you will begin to glean why things exist, rather than thinking they can't possibly have a place in your universe and thereby cutting yourself off from allowing all symbols that you create to be of service to you.

All creations—whether you create them consciously ("yes, I wish to create this") or you create them unconsciously ("something I heard as a child that followed me around in my life")—all creations have a positive purpose. When you deny this and call them extraneous, most obviously you do not look for this positive purpose, and you even create the negative effect from the negative designation that you assigned. However, all creations are symbols—only symbols, reflections of your consciousness. So therefore, when any creation, any symbol, presents itself, it gives you the opportunity to derive a positive message that will aid and assist you in your own growth. Sometimes what is negative may be so overwhelmingly obvious, or seem so overwhelmingly obvious, that individuals will say, "Well, there is not anything that could possibly be of any value in that creation." Here is where we beg to differ with you. There is simply no such thing as an extraneous creation. So therefore, use it all. There is simply nothing that you cannot use in a positive way. When it is most obvious what seems negative, that is not the idea—that is one idea, but not the idea that will allow you to transform the symbol you say you do not prefer. Allowing all your creations to be of service to you is what allows you to transform them to what you prefer.

So again, one of the phrases we sometimes use is: when you find yourself saying, "Why is this happening to poor little old me? I certainly did nothing to deserve this," always—always—you can simply turn it around and say, "How does this serve me?" And not only how does it serve me, but how does it serve me exactly as it already is? You will always, always come up with some answer. You may come up with many over what you label to be the course of time, but almost never will there not be something that is immediately obvious. Once you take what is positive from any creation and apply it, having learned the lesson from it, it simply has no reason to now exist in your life. There are again no extraneous creations. So when any creation delivers the message it was created in a positive way to give, it transforms. You got the idea; you accepted the exchange of symbols and ideas, and it transforms. When something seems to repeat—which by the way it literally never does, but we understand the notion that it seems to—you can understand you have not gotten the message and it is simply returning to give you that message, and returning, and returning, and returning. But the moment you get the message, it transforms. So therefore, you can use it all.

Therefore, that will be our present to you on this birthday at this timing. Allow me to thank you all for continuing to participate and co-create these interactions with our energy. In a sense you may say that I, Elan, am beginning this interaction solo, and this will be in that sense my first solo interaction in a group situation. Therefore, I thank you for your willingness to continue to view more than what is right in front of you. Your consciousness is what enables us to have in that sense the opportunity to communicate with you. You are doing us a great service. Just as many of you perceive that we are of service to you, you are no less perceived as being of service to us.
