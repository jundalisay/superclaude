We spoke about first through 4th density.

These densities are not laws or rigid parameters of existence. They are simply observations, descriptions, and very arbitrary.

However, they describe different densifications of consciousness.

For within All-That-Is are an infinite number of experiences and dimensionalities.

And so along these lines, there are an infinite number of densities. 

However, within any given framework, there are certain guidelines or classifications which are relevant within that framework.

From another framework, these may have very little or no relevance.

Therefore, this description of your octave of experience shall be relevant to you.
- It might not be relevant to other civilizations or to other ideas of dimensionality.

## 1st Density

This is point awareness, represented within a single point.

Therefore, that particular brand of consciousness is just a single point with no other reference.
- It has no other ability to compare
- It is a single pinpoint spot

On your planet, a close representation of this is your rock and mineral kingdom.

{{< n n="Ra and Willa say that minerals are 2nd density" >}}

Inorganic material is also a product consciousness.

You might feel that a rock does not have consciousness.

In reality, it is a very congealed solidified form of consciousness.

It most represents point consciousness, first density consciousness.

## 2nd Density 

This adds a second point. This creates a line awareness.

This is a more expanded view from singular point awareness as it allows some frame of reference, some idea of removal or comparison.

A second density consciousness would be represented by the plant and animal kingdom on your planet.

The 2nd point gives you the ability to look back.
- However, it is limited to simply looking back at the last point. 

<!-- Therefore, this is somewhat descriptive of the consciousness you have labeled upon your planet animal consciousness. -->

You are currently unable to relate to your plant kingdom. So we shall use your animal kingdom to represent 2nd density.

This is why animals have little or no concern for their future.

They simply exist in the now. 

However, they may look back at the last point at which they were at. Their limitation in looking back from whence they came is limited to the last point which they were at.
- This represents 2nd density.

## 3rd Density

You add the 3rd dimension by adding a 3rd point above that line that can view that line. 

This lets it view:
- where it is
- where it came from

It can see beyond the last point within the line. 

This is a more expanded view of consciousness. From the all-knowingness of All-That-Is, **this third point is the first separation.**

This first separation is again the separation of knowingness, the ability to be self-reflective within these parameters.

In your particular experience of third density, even though you do look back, even though you may reflect back upon yourself, there is still some seemingness of being a part of the immediate environment.

In fact, one of the ideas within that type of consciousness, particularly when just adding the third point from two-point type of consciousness, is the sensational feeling that you are being part of the environment, not in control of your participation or your view of your environment. 

Therefore, a 3rd density symptom is one being controlled or being a product of their environment.

One of the characteristics of 3rd density is the time continuum where:
- the present is a result of the past
- the present results in the future


## 4th Density

When you add the a 4th point, or the fourth density, you are now able to see through yourself, through the other three ideas. You realize that it all comes from you.

Therefore, there is less limitation.

The emphasis is then shifted toward the idea that you do create that entire dimensionality. This is a more expanded viewpoint with less separation.

When moving to the fourth density point of view, adding again that other aspect, removing yourself in a sense from the triangular volumetric experience, you are now able to see yourself from without and realize that it is all actually within. 

Therefore, all time is then seen as simultaneous. Therefore, the idea of past, the idea of future in a sense are still within experiential reality; however, it is all understood to be the idea of simultaneous. Therefore, any now moment has total validity, has total reality, has total manifestability. Therefore, there is no real per se continuum from one point to another other than the realization that that continuum is created in order to explore that type of linear time experience.

Therefore, again, you are still physical within the idea of fourth density, but the limitations that you have explored in third begin to fall by the wayside as you realize it is all a product of your own consciousness.


## 5th Density

When we add the idea of fifth density, the first resultant factor is non-physicality. There is no longer the need for a physical body.

In actuality, what occurs is you then view yourself as the dimension, as the dimension itself—one step further removed, expanded from the fourth density viewpoint. 

No need for physicality, no need for the idea of linear time, although still some understanding of that idea and the ability to interact within the parameters of that idea. You are again in fifth viewed as the dimension itself.


## 6th Density

This still has some separation. However, it includes you being many dimensions and not just the one that you had considered yourself to be.

There is less of the idea to identify with in order to explain, but this will roughly give you the idea of the difference as in these densities.

So sixth is viewing yourself as many dimensions with still some idea of separation.

In 7th density, you are now completely as multi-dimensional. 

You are simultaneously able to single anyone out in many dimensions but able to experience them simultaneously.

There are not many 7th density civilizations or consciousness in touch with you at this time, for that expanded consciousness has fairly little in common with your civilization, with your focus.

That describes the octave that is most close to your particular chosen focus.
