Your society has sent out the message of your existence to other societies, to other worlds, to extraterrestrial consciousness, and as well begin to scan back in that sense for the reply. 

You refer to this program as SETI, or Search for Extraterrestrial Intelligence. In that sense, your systems are, shall we say, scanning some of the more likely locations, some of the more likely stellar bodies you refer to as stars, for signs of life. And in that sense, one of the aspects that will allow your scientists to determine that perhaps an area is deserving of further investigation is what you sometimes refer to as redundancy or a redundant signal. For it is, shall we say, conceived that if something is repeating itself in a particular series in a particular way, that that repetition in a sense will represent intelligence and that that repetition will represent a message being sent, perhaps you can say, back to you.

Colloquially speaking, to extrapolate upon this idea, you can apply this very same concept to your actual life and the living of your life itself. For your higher self, if you wish to make the separation between your higher self and your physical self, perhaps you can say one way to look at it is, it sends messages or allows certain ideas to come to the awareness of your physical self along a timeline in a fashion that you would refer to as unfoldment. Therefore, you may understand in like fashion whereby your scientists are waiting for a redundancy or waiting for a repetition to begin to, in that sense, get the message from another society, that your higher self, much the same way, will seem to send messages to your physical self in a redundant form to allow you to identify that that particular notion, that particular idea that is repeating itself, has a significance, contains, perhaps you can say, a message.

So therefore, this can be applied in your life as follows: if you perceive that there are patterns that you create within your life, things perhaps which you do not prefer in any way, shape, or form, which seem to, quote unquote, "keep coming around," keep coming back in that sense, are a redundancy. You may understand by extension from the aforementioned analogy and in no uncertain terms that that redundancy, that repetition, is a message from your higher self. Your willingness to say, "Oh my goodness, here that comes again, woe is me," does not allow you to use and identify first of all the redundancy but second of all the message. It fixates you in what you may call victimhood: "Oh, everything happens to me," rather than allowing you to use all your creations in a positive way.

Individuals seem more than willing to use the positive-seeming creations in a positive way, but your line is drawn many times when you find that there is a redundancy of seemingly negative occurrences. "Well, I created the positive, but I would never create the negative." This particular attitude is, perhaps you can say, only taking half of the responsibility of creating your reality. And you truly do create it all. However, you do not create the negative-seeming occurrences for no reason; they are not superfluous. In fact, they are the results of notions that you have bought into which no longer serve you.

But in that you create your lives and define yourself by your past, when you buy into a particular idea and adopt and act that way, it becomes, in that sense, part of what you consider to be your mechanism or your automatic behavior. And the way that you can change that automatic behavior only will always begin by the acknowledgment of the belief that lies behind that behavior. For although your actions create your life in a physical world, the foundations for your actions are your beliefs, emotions, and thoughts. And only by tracing back to the fundamental belief which has you acting in a particular way, acknowledging that belief, assigning a new belief, extrapolating the actions that one would have with that new belief, and then performing those actions—the actions of the preferred belief, having acknowledged the old belief—only then can you change them and get the effect of changing them.

But because acknowledgment is the first step, your, shall we say, layered presentation of yourself constantly delivers messages to the forefront of your personality, to your conscious mind, in the form of redundant symbols, to allow you to see: "Look over here, look over here, look over here." If you look at the symbol, if you glean the meaning from it, if you derive how it serves you, then you have the belief that you can acknowledge and then change it. If you say, "Here that comes again," you shut down the mechanism. The denial of what keeps coming around simply again tucks it back into what you might call your subconscious closet, and it comes back knocking nastier and louder. For the idea is you are creating it; it is your child, it is screaming for your awareness—not to be obnoxious, not to annoy you, but to allow you to see, as one who is deciding to be a conscious creator, "Here is a belief that perhaps you have bought into. Perhaps you have bought into it out of not knowing there was any other belief. Perhaps you were spoon-fed that, not knowing again that there was an alternative. But here it is, you do have it. You do have it. I will show you, I will show you. When you acknowledge it once again, you own it. Then you can change it. When you deny it, you shut the door to the closet, and then the belief knocks louder."

This is why many times you will have what you call negative downward spirals in your life, and many times perceive that you have reached the bottom. However, this can all simply be reversed by, first of all, being aware of the fact that the redundancy is, all right to use your term, trying to tell you something. And that in the acknowledgment that you create your entire reality, your willingness to be receptive to what it is telling you is again the thing that will allow you to become aware of beliefs.

You say, "Well, I have a subconscious mind, there are many beliefs tucked within that subconscious mind, and I can't get to them. What will I do? How can I change?" Good question. The answer is to look at your life, to begin to again not only zealously use the positive but realize that what is being interpreted as negative is a creation and can also be used as well. Your willingness to approach the things that you don't prefer from the perspective of a creator results rather than saying "Why me?" in you asking, "All right, I do see what is negative about this, but I will ask, 'How does this serve me exactly the way it is? What does it show me about what I believe? This keeps coming around; I am ready to look at it. What is the belief? What must I believe to be experiencing this particular event?'" And this is a creator speaking. It is acknowledging that again perhaps there are some creations that you unfittingly created, but now you are willing to allow it to become conscious.

When you say, "I wish to access my subconscious mind," and then your life begins to bubble up with many redundant negative things, and then you cower and run, you display to the universe that perhaps you are not quite ready to look at what's in your subconscious closet. But I assure you that the timing of it presenting itself is your sign—that you always ask for—that you can handle it. For there is simply nothing within you that you can create that you cannot handle. And to extend this idea, anything in your reality can be used in a positive way. So if you say, "Well, I'm the type of person that attracts this type of relationship," as an example, "Why do I keep attracting it?" Keep attracting it. This can be applied across the board to any instance in your life and need not be, shall we say, simply narrowly applied to relationships. The beliefs present themselves, one by one, on a silver platter. Your willingness to acknowledge the negative beliefs which no longer serve you but which you are buying into and therefore acting like you believe them—your willingness to acknowledge them equalizes them to all other beliefs. The denial gives it more weight.

Now that I am acknowledging this, now that I am putting it out on the table, allow me to push it aside and put a few other options on the table, for I have equalized this to all other choices within me. Therefore, what is the belief that I prefer? Well, I prefer this—and state the belief.

So this is nothing that you need to brush up on your experts, I assure you.

And you do not run from the old belief; you have fully acknowledged it. But it is not running from, is not denying anything within you. For I remind you, though you present yourselves as physical, pointed, focused beings, that is but one aspect of who you are. You contain everything within the totality of who you are. So truly, you can run from nothing, for a) there is nowhere to go, and b) everything is already within you; there is nowhere to put them.

So it is not the recognition that you contain things that you do not prefer that can destroy you; it is the recognition that you will empower those things that you do not prefer within you. For obviously you contain it all. If you simply have a notion, you contain it, or you could not understand it. So you contain the potential of what you would call the most horrible of the outcomes on your planet, but you also contain the potential for the most sublime. And you need not deny that the horrible presentation exists; that is polarity, that is the exploration of positive and negative. But what you choose to focus on is what determines what you get.

But the point is they become irrelevant to your choices. The only reason they are now relevant to your choices is because no one has told you this, or you have not realized what I am saying consciously until now. But the simple notion, simply becoming aware of what I am saying for but a split second, gives you access to using all your creations in a positive way and allowing the negative ones not to go away but transform into the positive. Again, you cannot get rid of the negative; there is nowhere to put it. But you can choose and therefore experience only the positive.

Perhaps one of the first beliefs you may wish to look at, as if you truly believe that—for many individuals in and of themselves will simply say, "Well, you can only appreciate the positive by experiencing the negative to compare it to." So if you are willing to believe that you can balance off the positive and negative, and that is one truth, but you can also be aware of the negative and only experience the positive—that is just as true. Both of them are now on your table before you. I will allow you to choose whichever you want. But acting like you can experience all positive does take the form of only being willing to look at any circumstance from the perspective of how it serves you. Is this clear?
