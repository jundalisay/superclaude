Now if you have that ability then you do not necessarily need a specific representational tool that you are used to thinking is the only tool that represents abundance if you are still able to do what you need to do when you need to do it. So, you are exploring many different ways to create in your life what you truly need to do. But it does not exist everywhere. It does not exist on other worlds.

For an example, you may think, "I want to have this experience. I want to have this thing. In order to have that, I must have money. And in order to have the money, I must go and get a job. But you have taken all these steps, all these extra steps, you see.

So getting the money involves this whole other process over here that may take years and years and years when all the while if you had been willing to let your abundance serve you automatically and synchronistically you may have been able to turn around and find someone that's tapping you on the shoulder all the time saying but I want to give you this thing why are you so insistent on making all this money and taking years and years and years and years to get what I want to give you now.

So the idea is simply to trust that you are always abundant and that if money is the way that you can be abundant quickest then money is the way that will come to you. An opportunity will come into your life that will allow you to make the money or you will be given the money or you will get it somehow that will allow you to do what you need to do. But if you simply trust that you are abundant and follow at any given moment what excites you the most—and that is the most important key—what excites you the most. Then life will support you 100% as life has always supported you and it will allow you to attract into your reality whatever situations will allow you to do what you need to do when you need to do it.

And if money does not come with it then take that as a sign that money is not necessary for that particular interaction. If it were it would have been there first.

Follow what excites you the most in life. Even if you have been taught to believe it can't support you, it can. Excitement is the physical translation of the vibratory energy of the path you chose to be in this life. That's what excitement is. The energy that represents the path you chose to be. Therefore following at any given moment the situation that brings with it the highest degree of excitement is taking the path of least resistance, is living an effortless life, and it will bring into your life all the individuals and all the situations necessary to allow you to continue to be that effortless being.

That's the way life works and it does work that way when you let it. There is absolutely nothing too simple or too good to be true.


### Getting Out of Your Own Way/Denial

**Participant:** Doing what excites me the most. Doing what brings me the most joy at any given moment.

**Bashar:** At any given moment that you are capable with the tools you have at hand of acting upon.

**Participant:** So what I found out that there's times at which I seem to exist with levels of denial. All right. Be that certain programming, be ways of being. **Bashar:** Trust 100% trust in the idea you prefer to be rather than 100% trust in an idea you don't prefer to be. You did not have to do anything special in order to deserve to exist. Infinite creation obviously therefore believes you deserve to exist. Give yourself the same benefit of the doubt.

*All right. Sure. I'll accept.*

Understand? We are not telling you to do something you are not already doing. Each and every one of you has 100% trust in the idea you are experiencing in life. We are simply telling you that you can put that 100% trust behind any idea you prefer. And when you do, that's the physical reality you will get because you always get the physical reality that is representative of the idea you have 100% of your trust behind.

If you have 100% of your trust behind the idea that you will succeed sometimes but not too often, that's the reality you get. And then you say, "Well, what's wrong with me? I'm not functioning correctly." You are functioning perfectly. You are getting exactly the representation in physical reality of the idea you have 100% of your trust behind. If you don't like the definition of the reality you get, change the definition and put 100% of your trust behind that. Then that's the reality you'll experience. It is that simple. Simple mechanics, simple physics.

**Participant:** The patterns seem to be so rutful.

**Bashar:** If you say so, that's your belief. If the answer is no, then don't keep putting energy into the idea. "Well, it seems to be this way." If you truly are changed, if you truly are going to act like the person who has changed the definition of their reality, you won't say the things you used to say as the old person who believed you were in a rut. Because the new you knows there's no such thing. So don't talk that way if you are the new person.

You take absolutely no time to change because you do project a world of linear time reality. It may take a little time for the physical reality to transform into the representation of the real belief you are being. But don't let that let you believe that you haven't changed. You change the second you believe you have. Let your physical reality take whatever time is necessary to transform into a reflection of the fact that you have indeed changed.

Do not be impatient. The only reason that you—any of you—create impatience is because you think you need patience and you don't. If you are enjoying every single moment of your life because you know every single moment of your life is there for a reason, then you won't be waiting for something better to come along. You will be enjoying what you are doing now. And when you are not waiting for something better to come along, you don't need to be patient. And if you don't need to be patient, you don't create impatience.

So if you know you have changed, if you are able to recognize that you have the ability to consciously—there is no mystery in this. Consciously decide choose: "I am now different. This belief such and such is now what I am." Act like it. If you do not see your physical reality on the surface change immediately, don't immediately fall back and say, "Oh, well, I failed. Oh, well, I guess I'm no good at this. Oh, well, I guess I can't really change myself. Oh, well, it's too difficult to change."

Why give up that soon? Recognize, first of all, that it takes a little bit of time for the physical reality to transform. However, no physical symbol and no physical situation or circumstance has any built-in meaning. They don't mean anything. The things, the circumstances, the props in your life don't have built-in meaning. You give them meaning and the meaning you give them is the effect you get out of it.

So if you know you have changed and you see something, a situation occur in your life that on the surface appears to be the same or similar to something that used to occur, why simply assume that you haven't changed? "Oh well look here it happens again. I am in the same rut over and over and over again." No, you are continuing to believe that the surface appearance has the same meaning and it doesn't.

If you are different, it is different. Give it a new meaning. Give the meaning to the situation that you would imagine a person who is different in the way you want to be different would look at it. Do not look at it the way you used to be. Look at it the way you are now. Say to yourself, "Well, look, if I was really this different person, if I was really this person I want to be, all abundant and all of this. If I were that person, how would I look at this situation? Would I not see something positive in it? Would I not have a different perspective, a different point of view? Yes, you would. So, look at it that way, not the way you used to."

By looking at it the way you used to, you constantly reinforce the old reality. And that's what creates the rut. Simply because you assume that just because a situation on the surface appears to be the same as it used to be, that it has the same reason for being there or has the same meaning built into it. No situation has built-in meaning. All situations are fundamentally neutral, blank, empty, zero. The meaning you give to it based upon what you have been taught to believe it represents is exactly the effect you get out of it. Positive meaning in, positive effect out; negative meaning in, negative effect out. It is up to you. Again, simple physics, simple mechanics.
