## Introduction: Imagination and Creating Reality

All of you generally realize you have an imagination, but most of you have never been taught what the imagination is really for. Why it really exists, how you can really use it in connection to the creation of your experiential reality.

In other words, some of you already understand and some of you may not understand, but the fundamental to everything we will share with you is the discussion of the idea that each and every one of you individually and collectively are absolutely and utterly responsible for the creation of your experiential day-to-day reality. Your physical reality, it's your creation, your projection.

You have been taught for thousands of your years, tens of thousands of your years, that what you call physical reality exists outside of you. That you exist within it. That it is separate from you. That you in a sense are separate from it. But even your own scientists now are beginning to discover it is not. They are now beginning to be aware of what I have been sharing with you. That what you call seemingly exteriorized physical reality is actually a projection of what you imagine it to be. That physiological reality does not really empirically exist apart from your definition of it.

That physiological reality, what you have for so long believed to be the solid exterior world, actually is created out of the definition. The idea therefore to understand how strong an impact definitions and belief have in creating that physiological reality.

All that you experience and what you call your day-to-day life is the product of what you have been taught to buy as a belief about what it ought or ought not to be. Only these definitions are what give physical reality its seeming solidity. Physiological solidity is also only one of the definitions that create your experience of physical reality. Physical reality can be very malleable, very fluctuable, very flexible, does not have to be as seemingly unchanging or as slowly changing as many of you have thought that it is. This definition of slow change of solid matter is simply the product of thousands of generations in your society of the habit of thinking of it that way.

But now, now, now you are awakening as a society to the exploration of different levels of what you call consciousness and spirituality, and are beginning to explore and recognize that you have many more levels — I shall say for now — of consciousness than you previously were taught that you have. You are beginning to discover different aspects of yourself in a way that allows you to think differently beyond what you are used to using as your senses. You are now discovering you have the capacity to sense more than you used to think you have. And in this application, in this exploration, you are now beginning to realize many things about the connectedness between your consciousness and physical reality that you have heretofore not understood exist. That consciousness and physical reality are not really separate ideas, but that physical reality — what you call physical reality — is but an expression of the consciousness that you are. One expression, and you have many of them, on many different levels, in many different dimensions of experience, and they are all real and all true expressions of yourself, of the consciousness, the total being that you are, of which this personality is but a fragment and aspect, an expression, one facet of the multi-dimensional crystal that each and every one of you are, individually and collectively.

Therefore, the majority of what we will be discussing this evening of your time will all basically be laid upon this understanding, this foundation, that you create your own reality, and will basically all be directed, generally speaking, into the understanding of your imagination. For it is your imagination that is the prime tool, the prime ingredient out of which and through which you can learn: A, that you have always created your reality through it, and B, that you can create any reality you desire from this point forward by getting in touch with how you have done it in the past — what the mechanism of your imagination is actually all about, and how you can use it consciously to create the reality you want.

## The Mechanism of Imagination

Imagination — it is the prime tool. It is the dimension of all experience, of all possible reality you could create yourself to live. Fundamentally speaking, it works as follows.

You have a picture in your mind of something you would like to do. A visualization, you call it. Now, this visualization will usually contain a picture of yourself doing the thing you want to do. You will daydream it, as you say. You will picture it, visualize it, imagine it. You will see yourself doing the things, having the things that you want.

Well, recognize something that you may not have recognized before. Your society has taught you, well, all right, that's all well and good, that's your imagination. Your society usually says that's just your imagination, and that picture you have isn't really real. Oh, it's a nice thought, a nice wish. Oh, but face reality — reality is not like that. But you have been taught to believe it's not. You have been taught to believe your imagination is not real.

But understand this: your imagination, what you call this visualization, this picture in your mind's eye, this seeing of yourself in a situation you would prefer to be in, is the viewing, the observation, of a real reality — another probable you. I say probable only because you don't believe it actually exists. It does exist. It is another you.

And if you begin to understand that it is a real reality, then what you can simply do is recognize that it represents another vibration, another frequency, which is the only thing that defines one reality from another. And if you recognize the difference between the frequency that the picture represents and the frequency you have been living in, then you may realize something else. If you wish that picture in your mind's eye to be the real reality, the experiential reality of your day-to-day life, then all you have to really do is watch the you in that picture doing the things you say you would prefer to do, and mimic the you in the picture in terms of its attitude, in terms of its approach to things, in terms of how that you — you say you want to be — would handle the circumstances and situations that have occurred in your life.

If a situation comes along and you don't know what to do with it, create the you in your imagination that you prefer to be. Hand the situation to that you and watch, in your meditative state, what the you — the idealized preferable you in your imagination — does with that situation. Watch the attitude. Watch how that you handles the situation. Watch, daydream out how that you will allow that situation to come to a successful conclusion. Then mirror it, match it, mimic it, and you will get the same conclusion because you will be being that you.

That's what your imagination is for: to create the template and the blueprint that will show you beyond a shadow of a doubt what methodology will work for you to be able to transform any situation, any circumstance, into the outcome and manifestation you prefer it to be.

If you trust that in your imagination what you are seeing is a real you and you start acting like that you, you will have the life of that you. You will be that person literally, immediately, and you will get the same outcome. You will get the same effect that you see in your imagination.

This is not a philosophical discussion. This is not an analogy. This is an actual mechanical, physical description of how it works. It is that easy. It is that simple.

We are discussing physics. We are discussing mechanics of how you create your physical reality. You do this all the time, anyway. It is simply that your society, not believing that your imagination is real, has allowed you to forget to place in your unconscious self the idea of the imagination, the ideas, the beliefs, the emotions that create your reality. When you're not in touch consciously with what those beliefs and imaginings are, then you do not necessarily see the connection between your outer, exterior, experiential, day-to-day reality and imagination.

This understanding, this connection, this allowance, this remembering that your physical reality is created by your belief, through your imagination — whether consciously or unconsciously — allows you to use it in a conscious way, to know beyond the shadow of a doubt, clearly, transparently, that you create your physical reality; to watch it happen, to watch yourself functioning consciously moment by moment, day by day. And to know that you are, each and every one of you, an expression of the infinite, an expression, an aspect of creation.

Your own literature tells that you are made in the image of God. What does that mean? It means, from our perspective, that you are a reflection of the infinite creation. And if you are a reflection of the infinite creation, if the infinite creation is a multi-dimensional being, a multi-dimensional creator, then if you are made in that image, you yourself must be a multi-dimensional being, a multi-dimensional creator. You have that power. You are that power.

All beings within creation are all the different ways that the infinite creation has of expressing itself, through the imagination. It is all of you, all of us, are all the different ways that the infinite has of expressing itself through its imagination. You have the same capacity within your own experiential universe. That is how you do create your experiential universe, because you are co-creators with the infinite.

It is this that we have come to reflect to you, to remind you, so that you, in taking back your power, in acknowledging the aspect of the infinite creator that you are, can live, in a sense, as you were meant to live, which is in absolute ecstasy and joy. No one has to struggle. You kind of create it if you wanted. You have created it perhaps out of the belief that you need it to. But now you are waking up. Now you are beginning to realize that no one has to struggle, no one has to suffer. You are now realizing that ecstasy is what you are created from, and it is your birthright. Nothing is too good to be true. Nothing is too simple to be true unless you believe it must be discovered — then, believe me, you will get it. You will always get in your experiential reality the thing you believe, or have been taught to believe, is the most likely reality you will get. Getting in touch with that allows you to pick the reality you prefer.

Now at this time we extend to you our deepest appreciation in allowing us to interact with you this day of your time. To us, you are a gift. We learn from you. Everyone learns, everyone grows. It is unending through you. Our civilizations are given an opportunity to see the many more ways that the infinite creation can express itself to me. Therefore, we thank you for functioning as ambassadors to your society, for that is what you are right now, literally, and we thank you for allowing us to function as ambassadors of our respective world in the association of worlds. For bit by bit and day by day, through these interactions, we may co-create with each other a conducive atmosphere that will allow us to interact more openly, more blatantly, face to face. Our faces externally may be different, for our hearts and our souls are the same. And it is on that level that we can relate, and that anything can be understood.

In return for your willingness to co-create this interaction with us this day of your time, I ask you now: how may I serve you? Thank you.

### The Formula: Clarity of Intention, Definition, Desire, Deservability, Action

Here is the whole formula as it basically stands:

**Clarity of intention** — what is your overriding intention?

**Clarity of definition** — how are you defining the terminology of your overriding intention? What are the definitions of these states of being that you say you do and don't prefer? Be clear on what they mean to you, what they represent to you.

For example, again, some individual may say, "I wish to be rich, I wish to be rich, I wish to be rich," and they are not getting abundant — they are not getting that at all. Then if they examine their definition of what it means to be abundant, all of a sudden it may dawn on them that they have been taught, or they have taught themselves through life, to associate the idea of being rich with also carrying a personality trait that they don't want. Perhaps they have observed individuals in life that are rich that perhaps act in matters they don't want to act in, and they have made, for some reason, an assumption that if I am rich I have to act that way because all the rich people I see act that way. So they are connecting something they don't want with something they do. In clarifying the definition they can say: wait a minute, why must I assume that just because I might be rich I would have to act that way? I don't have to assume that. Now that I am clear on what my definition has been — the assumption that to be rich is also to act in a way I don't want to — now I can redefine it. I can understand I can be rich in the way I want to be rich, and I don't have to be any way I don't want to be, once the definition is clear. You can allow the reality to manifest more quickly because it is only your definition, very often, that keeps the reality away, because you might assume it contains something you don't want along with something you do.

**Clear desire** — in other words, do you really want it? Are you really sure you want it? Again, some individuals may not absolutely be sure they want the thing they say they want. Here's another example. An individual may have within them a belief that once they get what they want, why — no more excitement, no more excitement of the chase. And if they believe that more strongly than the excitement of getting it, then they will always just prevent themselves from getting it because they believe that trying to get it is more exciting, because they believe that once they get it, that's it. If you always understand there will always be more, you won't have to worry about that. But that may be one reason why an individual, even though they say "I want it, I want it, I want it," may not really clearly desire it.

**Clear deservability** — do you believe you deserve what you say you want? That's a big issue for many of you. Many of you do not think that you have a right to have what you desire, because your society has steeped many of you in notions of guilt, in notions of worthlessness, in notions of having to struggle in order to earn happiness.

**Clear action** — after you have a clear intention, clear definition, clear desire, clear deservability, the only thing that really remains is clear action. And all that simply means is, once you know what your intention, definition, desire, and deservability are, acting with conviction on the opportunities that come into your life that excite you is all it takes to bring you what you want.
