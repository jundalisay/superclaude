## The laws of psychic functioning

We will outline briefly a few of the laws of psychic functioning. 

### 1. Everything is one thing. That is how you are able to be connected to anything at all.

Identification, the matching of a frequency, will put you strongly in touch with that thing.

However, if certain conditions aren't met, you will not get a manifestation of psychic or telepathic functioning.

### 2. **The great need or desire to know the information**

If Adam doesn't care what Bob had for breakfast it is unlikely that Adam can generate an identical enough wavelength of interest with Bob to know what Bob had for breakfast.
