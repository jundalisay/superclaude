And even if it is, that stage of your technological evolution usually does not last that long. For very quickly in terms of history do you pass through the radio stage and go on to other levels, other sources of energy, other utilizations of energy for your communication systems.

The idea of our existence is not forced upon them. Until such time as they exhibit the willingness to communicate in that way. Until their entire society understands that they can communicate in a loving way, not in a way that will in that sense destructure their society in a negative way causing chaos as you say and panic, or the disassemblement of their society through fear, doubt, self-doubt that we might be something greater than you that might undermine your own abilities to believe in yourselves. Testing the waters as you say to see what level of belief system still exists, what level of fear still exists in your society. Testing individuals here and there to see whether it is now truly more conducive for your world and our worlds to interact face to face as equals in blatant ways. And as we have indicated, these times are approaching. Within what you call the next two to three decades, this can be a relatively common thing.
