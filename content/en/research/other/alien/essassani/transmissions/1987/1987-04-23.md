### The Mechanical Analogy of Control

All connections, linkages, and control—if you wish, that is your entitlement. Imagine if you will that each and every circumstance, situation, relationship, an event in your life is like what you call a wheel before you, a gear if you wish. And let us say you have many such gears poking into your particular sphere of influence, your particular immediate reality. These gears can be seen to be opportunities or circumstances that are going on in your life, ideas that you are aware of that exist in your reality that you can act upon, can do something about if you wish.

Now envision that while you may have your own bubble of reality and these gears may be poking through the skin of that bubble to you, that they are connected to a rod, a shaft if you will. Now recognize that these gears are connected to other gears which are connected to shafts which are connected to other gears in many, in fact all, different directions and planes of existence. So simply recognize that when you turn any of the gears in your bubble, all the gears are turning. Now even though this may not be literal in terms of a mechanical structure, it is literal in terms of the effect being the same as if it were a literal mechanical structure. When you turn any gear, any situation, any opportunity in any direction that exists within your bubble, you move all other gears. So as you make any change in the format that is indicative of your immediate reality, then must be changes in the formats of all other reality, all other reality, to maintain the balance of the changes you have made. It must be so.

Even though as we have said this is not necessarily a literal blueprint of the structure of the physical Universe, in effect it is literal because you get the same effect as if it were truly literally a mechanical structure in that analogy. So simply recognize that you have all the control right there within your bubble. The circumstances that make themselves obvious to you are your gears to move around, and you can then know that by simply turning those gears, because of the linkages and the connections to all other gears, you from being just right where you are affect the entire universe. You have an impact on all the way the rest of the universe shapes itself to be.

That is also why you have the best control—I shall use your term "best" for now—the best control when you remain in the present where all of your control gears are. When you go outside that sphere looking in the past, looking in the future, looking hither thither as you say, then you are ignoring the fact that within your own bubble, I shall say, of reality, all the control mechanisms are present and at hand literally. And they are simply the opportunities themselves that present themselves to you in that reality, the ones that represent the things that excite you the most, as we have discussed with you many times. And when you act upon them, when you turn them in the direction you prefer—positive, negative, right, left if you wish—that determines utterly the balance that is created in the remainder, so to speak, of the universe. In the reflection of the universe, for the reflection that is out there are all the other gears that must by definition respond to the turn of your gear. So what you do determines the feedback you create, the circumstances you create, the environment you create in the reality around you, because you are turning the gear. It is that simple. Keep it that simple.

This simple mechanical analogy will allow you to understand that you have infinite control from right where you are to what you perceive to be the infinite reaches of the universe. For none of that is really in your terms truly "out there." It is all right here.
