### The Mechanical Analogy of Control

All connections, linkages, and control—if you wish, that is your entitlement. In recently speaking with a member of your Society, we did pull forth out of our interaction the recognition that we can now find a great, perhaps benefit, in sharing at this time with you of the idea of the degree to which you have control over the linkages and connections of every circumstance and situation in your life to every other circumstance and situation in life itself.

And by drawing what you may call a mechanical analogy, it may assist in forming a picture in your mentality that is most conducive to a complete and simple fundamental understanding of the way in which the mechanism works that allows you to know you are in control and that all circumstances are linked and connected to all other circumstances—an idea I have discussed many times with you, but perhaps the simplicity of this mechanical analogy will further illuminate the idea.

Imagine if you will that each and every circumstance, situation, relationship, an event in your life is like what you call a wheel before you, a gear if you wish. And let us say you have many such gears poking into your particular sphere of influence, your particular immediate reality. These gears can be seen to be opportunities or circumstances that are going on in your life, ideas that you are aware of that exist in your reality that you can act upon, can do something about if you wish.

Now envision that while you may have your own bubble of reality and these gears may be poking through the skin of that bubble to you, that they are connected to a rod, a shaft if you will. And that these rods, these shafts, in proceeding through the skin of your bubble to the outside of your bubble into other reality bubbles—all the potential circumstances and situations that there can be in all levels of dimensional reality—they also on the other end of that shaft have a gear, a corresponding gear like the one that is in your bubble.

Now recognize that these gears are connected to other gears which are connected to shafts which are connected to other gears in many, in fact all, different directions and planes of existence. So simply recognize that when you turn any of the gears in your bubble, all the gears are turning. Now even though this may not be literal in terms of a mechanical structure, it is literal in terms of the effect being the same as if it were a literal mechanical structure. When you turn any gear, any situation, any opportunity in any direction that exists within your bubble, you move all other gears. Now some of those gears in your terms may be bigger, some may be smaller, so some may move more infinitesimally than others, some may move greatly, but you move them all nonetheless.

So recognize what this means to you: You do not have to go far and wide searching for the way to control those outside gears, searching and searching for years to gain control over gears in that sense which you believe are so very far removed from your immediate reality. You do not have to go searching because you are already in control of them all. The gears, all the situations that poke into your sphere, already connects you to all those other gears. Turn them any way you wish, it will turn all the other gears.

Now many of you may then find that by this analogy what will allow you to understand that as you move, polarity is balanced always throughout the Universe by everything that you do. It is once again to understand this as a mechanical analogy, for if you have a gear on your end of the shaft and a gear on the other end of the shaft, if you—to your perception in facing the gear—turn it, let us say, one quarter turn to the right, recognize that the other hypothetical individual on the other end will see that gear turn a quarter turn to their left. You understand the perspective? As such, they are moving the same way, but from one perspective they are turning to your right and from the other perspective they are turning to your left. This is a mechanical analogy of why balance is maintained in the universe, because everything is connected literally. So as you make any change in the format that is indicative of your immediate reality, then must be changes in the formats of all other reality, all other reality, to maintain the balance of the changes you have made. It must be so.

Even though as we have said this is not necessarily a literal blueprint of the structure of the physical Universe, in effect it is literal because you get the same effect as if it were truly literally a mechanical structure in that analogy. So simply recognize that you have all the control right there within your bubble. The circumstances that make themselves obvious to you are your gears to move around, and you can then know that by simply turning those gears, because of the linkages and the connections to all other gears, you from being just right where you are affect the entire universe. You have an impact on all the way the rest of the universe shapes itself to be.

That is also why you have the best control—I shall use your term "best" for now—the best control when you remain in the present where all of your control gears are. When you go outside that sphere looking in the past, looking in the future, looking hither thither as you say, then you are ignoring the fact that within your own bubble, I shall say, of reality, all the control mechanisms are present and at hand literally. And they are simply the opportunities themselves that present themselves to you in that reality, the ones that represent the things that excite you the most, as we have discussed with you many times. Those are the control gears. And when you act upon them, when you turn them in the direction you prefer—positive, negative, right, left if you wish—that determines utterly the balance that is created in the remainder, so to speak, of the universe. In the reflection of the universe, for the reflection that is out there are all the other gears that must by definition respond to the turn of your gear. So what you do determines the feedback you create, the circumstances you create, the environment you create in the reality around you, because you are turning the gear. It is that simple. Keep it that simple.

This simple mechanical analogy will allow you to understand that you have infinite control from right where you are to what you perceive to be the infinite reaches of the universe. For none of that is really in your terms truly "out there." It is all right here. You have pushed the illusion "out there" to make it seem as if you are operating within space, a volume of space, but that is simply the illusion of this particular physical Universe. It is all right here. And in a sense, truly it is all one Gear with two sides, though you can recognize the analogy is exactly the same if you remove the linkage and simply allow that far gear to simply be the other side of the gear you have your hand on. You get the same effect: turn to the right, somebody standing on the other side still sees it turning to the left. It is the same gear. Truly it is the same gear. Allow yourselves to know you are in gear. Allow yourselves to know you are in the driver's seat. You have all the control right at home, here, now, in the presence.
