## The Sassani Planet

Our planet does not really have widespread seasonal changes. Our axis tilt is not as strong as your own. 

Our overall environment, in your terminology, is relatively similar from moment to moment, not usually ranging beyond what you would call an approximate span of about 13 to 15 of your degrees, one way or another.

The idea, however, on your world, of your season, your times of change, your autumn represents — as many of you already instinctively understand — a gathering together, a going within, a nurturing energy of introspection. The idea of harboring new understandings, new discoveries of yourself within yourself, surrounding the inner hearth of your being with all the ideas that you have discovered yourself to be during the active months of your year. And now coming into an assessment, a type of early hibernation — which in no way, shape, or form implies that you become inactive, but simply that your mentality, your consciousness, collectively and sometimes even individually, can begin to recognize that this particular timing in your world represents the idea of the opening and the exploration of many of the things you have gathered, many of the packages you have picked up along the way to this point — to review, and allow yourself to perceive what within those experiences, within those packages, represents the things that you prefer to carry through into your new year, and those things that you prefer to allow to assimilate within an old idea and, in that sense, to remain within the concept of the past.

It is now timing in your society, in general, for the opening of many spirit doors within you, many levels of consciousness — now not so much directly connected with physiological reality, but now the exploration of many states of dream reality, dream awareness, to begin to be able to allow more blending between the non-physical and the physical to occur. So that upon your birthing into a new year, you are stepping through that gate as a completely whole description of yourself — both physical and non-physical aspects of your consciousness in tandem, connected side by side, stepping through into that event of a new year with all the unfoldment of new growth, new perspectives, new discovery.

But now your autumn time, as you have all created mythologically, is recognized as the time of communion with spirit — in that sense, the spirit of yourself within yourself, and all aspects, in whatever way your consciousness, the spirit you are, can present itself to you, both positive and negative mirrors, for you to gaze into, to discover those things you desire within yourself to discover, those things you can integrate into the being you prefer to be.
