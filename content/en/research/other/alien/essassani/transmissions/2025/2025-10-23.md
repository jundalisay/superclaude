There is a civilization that existed off the coast of the ancient continent of Mu.

This civilization was a very special civilization that focused on blending cosmic genetics with human terrestrial genetics. So, this was a civilization of hybrid beings. 

They all had blue skin. They were taller in stature. 

They possessed a great deal of psychic abilities. 

This civilization evolved independently of Lemuria, or ancient Mu. 

This is because the focus there was almost entirely spiritual in nature.

These beings were designed to absorb as much of the celestial energies as possible and then, acting as human anchors for those energies, they could transmit those frequencies through their body into the Earth, thus distributing those energies throughout the leyline network. 

This allowed for a greater synchronization to take place between humanity and the variety of celestial beings that were guiding humanity and working with humanity during those days.

This island civilization then began to spread out, and they brought the teachings that they had received to a variety of different tribal and indigenous populations. 

These beings are described in your ancient texts as the Seven Sages. We share this with you because they still exist. They are still here. They have not left.

Even though they are described as seven, those seven represent many beings from that civilization.

We will share a name for this group so that way they can be called upon for reference, for assistance, for clarification, and for guidance: Anu Deva. This is a name that can be used to access them.
