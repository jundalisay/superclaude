### Journaling Technique for Spirit Communication


Let us begin with techniques to hone the skill of receiving and perceiving communications from the spirit realm. First, take fifteen quiet minutes in the morning or evening. Sit quietly with a journal and mentally ask questions of your spirit guides. Write down whatever you get without worrying if it comes from your own mind or your spirit team. Then relax, listen, feel, and write down whatever you receive, even if it seems nonsensical. Do this twice a day for two to three weeks without distraction. You will start seeing coherence, relevance, and patterns in what you write. Do not analyze where it comes from; just keep writing. This is how you practice discernment between your thoughts and communications from your spirit team. Over two to three weeks, the difference will become distinct, and answers will become clearer. You will realize communication has always been occurring. The information you receive will indicate how you have already been receiving guidance through synchronicity and other methods. Refinements will occur, and information will come from different beings on your spirit team, becoming more complex and precise based on your questions. This allows you to practice a spectrum of communications on topics you are passionate about. Do not judge or analyze; just write. After three weeks to a month, reviewing your journal will reveal a progressive pattern and clarity. You may even sense specifically where information originates. You might also receive communications from animals or elemental beings globally. Be open to receiving without labeling or judging. Whether it is a single word or a paragraph, write whatever comes. If you are earnest and stay in these quiet segments, you will hone your sensitivity. You may receive valuable information for your life path, gain insights into yourself, and understand how to be of service to humanity.
