Physical reality is a reflection of your state of being, your state of consciousness. 

There really is nothing outside of you. You're not really in physical reality; physical reality is within you. It's within your consciousness. It is a concept that you are experiencing of yourself from a certain perspective. 

However, we understand that the way in which you have chosen to experience your particular dimension of physical experience is as though you are within a realm of space and time, as you call it. 

But what is space? What is time?

They are illusionary, but you still create the effect of them — the experience of them in your life experience as a physical being, a physicalized being, so to speak — where you have taken your energy, the high frequency energy of your spirit nature, your non-physical nature, and in a sense have lowered that frequency, crystallized that frequency down into what you call the experience, the matrix of the structure of physical reality."

This is going on within your consciousness. 

You're not actually in any place really called physical reality; it's all within. Nevertheless, we understand that the way you chose to experience this makes it appear to be outside of you. So the thing to remember in order to give yourself more opportunity to choose and experience the kind of physical reality you prefer — the kind of physical reality that you say is more in alignment with your true vibration — is to begin to see and experience physical reality as reflection, similar to the idea of the reflection that exists in your glass mirrors. You know that when you look at your reflection in a glass mirror, you know that you're not really over there, and you know that if you want to change the reflection in the glass mirror, you don't go to the reflection and attempt to make it change. You must change yourself in order to see the change in the reflection."

"So physical reality, being a reflection, being a Hall of Mirrors, operates in much the same kind of modality. Any change you wish to see in the so-called reflection — the outer reality — must begin within. Must begin within the inner reality, which is you, within your consciousness, within your vibration, within your state of being."

"Now we understand that in having created this experience called physical reality and having imposed the idea of space and time upon your consciousness, that you have created also the concept called forgetfulness — where you have forgotten that physical reality is not real. But now in this day and age, you are beginning to remember. That's what this exploration of consciousness, as you call it, is really all about. You're beginning to remember who and what you really are as a being, as a consciousness — and that you are creating, projecting this experience you call physical reality — that it's coming from within you. And so the more you begin to explore this, you find yourself attracted to a myriad of protocols and rituals and tools and techniques, and as we say, permission slips, to give you the opportunity to remind yourselves that you are in fact creating all of this — that it's not really out there, it's not happening to you, it's happening through you."

"So in these kinds of interactions, in this kind of download of information, we offer you yet one more perspective — a series of ideas, a series of thoughts, a series of points of view that can be used, can be applied in your reality, in your day-to-day physical reality, to aid and assist you in changing in ways that you prefer to, in experiencing life the way you prefer to."

"First of all, please understand that before we talk about the idea of these changes, and before we talk about the idea of what your mentality is, what your mind is, what your ego is, how you're having this experience, how you're framing it, how you're setting it up, we would like to remind you about the issue of change. Because the idea is not that you have to learn to change. You change all the time. As we have said, you change billions — literally billions — of times per second. So quickly, so automatically, so fluidly, so smoothly, so effortlessly, you have forgotten that you're changing all the time. You have forgotten that the person you may be one moment is not the person you are in the next billionth of a second, or the billionth of a second after that, or after that, or after that. You literally become different people billions of times a second. Literally — this is not a metaphor. Literally, you become different people billions of times a second."

"However, the shifts that you make, the changes that you're making automatically all the time, are happening so smoothly, and the changes within one shift to another are so, in a sense, unnoticeable generally by you, that you think you're still the same person. You create this illusion, this fluid illusion of continuity. Thus then, the ideas that you think you're not really changing very much. But the fact is you're changing totally and completely billions of times a second. So the idea within all of this is not that you have to learn how to change; the idea is to learn how to define the next change so that it is different enough so that you can see the difference between the new change and the old one, and by that difference reveal that you have changed immediately, instantaneously, and automatically — and to do that more and more and more, so that you can understand that change is natural to you, it's happening all the time. It's a natural law of creation. All things change — except the first three laws, of course."

"The idea in that sense is that you exist — that's the first law. The second law: the One is All, the All is One — that's the second law. The third law: what you put out is what you get back — that's the third law. The fourth law is: Everything Changes — except the first three laws. And that's it. That's all any experience is based on — is those four laws. There are no others. A combination of these ideas would give you every experience and every level and every dimension of being."

"Now, as we asked, what is spacetime? Cursorily, it is in a sense, as we said, a structure imposed upon your consciousness — upon the unbroken consciousness of your being, your spirit, your soul if you wish. The spacetime structure itself is your ego. There is no difference between what you call your physical ego mind and the space-time structure. The very existence, the very creation of spacetime, of creating the idea of time and space, generates the ego itself, because you need that perspective in order to experience spacetime."

"An analogy would be, as we have said many times, the idea of a diving mask to explore under the water so you can see clearly. By going into physical reality, by changing your frequency to have a physical reality experience, it's like diving into your ocean without the idea of a mask — everything would be very murky. You need the focus so you can see clearly in the new realm, the new environment that you have projected yourself into, that you have in that sense dived into. So the ego is that mask. The ego is that lens through which you will experience the idea of physical reality. The ego's job, in a sense, the ego's definition, is just to keep you focused in the physical reality experience. Without that, you would not be capable of making sense out of the vibrations you call physical reality. So that is why the ego structure is part and parcel of that nature of that space-time structure — it's one and the same thing. As you create a space-time structure, you create an ego structure in order to experience it. They go hand in hand; they're simultaneously coexistent and co-created."

"Now when we say ego structure, again we don't necessarily mean it in the negative context, because we understand that many people on your planet experience the ego structure in what you call a negative way. The ego's basic job is not negative; in a sense, it's not even really positive per se — it's just neutral. It's just to keep you focused so you can have a physical experience. When the ego structure is given the label or defined as something that must do all of the creating in that experience, when it is given, in a sense, more than it was designed to do, when you stretch it and strain it beyond its designed tolerances, so to speak, then it starts to fight back. And then it creates and generates the idea of a personality out of control, in fear, resisting the natural self, because it no longer believes there is anything but the ego structure, because the ego structure has been given so much energy, so much focus, it becomes the overwhelming, overriding vibration of your life, and it blots out the idea of your ability to remember that you are spirit, to reconnect with the higher mind, and so on and so forth. "One way to clarify some of the ideas of physical reality and the linear space-time framework you've created is to, for the purposes of the illustration, for the purposes of the analogy, eliminate the space-time reference so that you can create a perspective, and by that perspective, by that difference of having no space-time, see what is going on in space-time. So let's take this analogy from the spirit perspective — this analogy of, quote-unquote, building a house."

"You all know that in spirit, because time does not exist in the same way, you can do anything in any order. And then you could say, 'Now I would like to experience the experience of building a house after living in the house.' And poof, there you are, building a house. From the linear space-time perspective, seemingly backwards, making no sense — 'Well, how can you live in the house before you build the house? I don't get it.' But the point of this illustration is to point out that these are individual events that really have no continuity connection. Each is its own experience. And the confusion will disappear when you define them as their own experiences, having no connection to another experience, except the connection you create — which is another experience unto itself."

"So even though we understand that generally speaking in linear space-time you go about building a house in order to create a house, you are actually not using the building of the house — not really, not mechanically — to actually create the house. But you make a connection between the two because of the belief system framework within your consciousness, within your ego structure."

"If you can begin to look at every single moment as its own reality for its own sake, with its own definition of being a moment equal to all other moments of experience, you can start to break this continuity experience down. That's something they believe they require, that they prefer, in order to learn certain things, to grow as a being, to grow as a spirit, to grow as a consciousness."

"When you understand it that way, then it's all well and good to know that you're creating the experience of that leading to the house. But what we're describing here is the actual mechanical reality: the building of the house and the living in the house are two completely different events that are not really created in that sense to be linked until you link them."

"Let us give you another analogy, perhaps again to help clarify this issue. You are at one of your fine Earth restaurants. The server comes up to you and says, 'What would you like to drink?' You order your drink. 'May I interest you in an appetizer?' You order your appetizer. 'What would you like as your main course?' You order your main course. 'Can I interest you in a little dessert?' You order your dessert. 'Would you like some coffee, tea, or an after-dinner drink?' You order that and are satisfied with the continuity of your meal from beginning to end."

And you go, 'Well, I suppose I could.' 'And then would you like dessert or would you like the appetizer last, or in what order would you actually prefer the meal?' Well, you know, now that you mentioned it, I guess I could change some things up into a different order. I guess I don't have to go by that structure. Each portion of the meal is its own thing and does not have to be ordered in order."

"The idea again is that each experience in your reality can be shuffled around. It's just that more often than not you don't prefer to because you've agreed to abide by certain consensus reality rules that give you the opportunity to create with your belief systems certain kinds of events, certain kinds of experiences that will give you an understanding of creation, an understanding of what you call process — the process of creation, the experience of the act of creation, rather than the simple instantaneous simultaneous existence of something. This is one of the definitions of physical reality — is the experience of that act of creation."

"But we understand that you can become confused about how one may seem to lead to the other, but they don't. Because that's what's happening when you look at an event as a means to an end — you are labeling them in ways that connect them in continuity. But the idea again is you cannot really look at negotiation as a means to an end. Negotiation is an opportunity. Since many of you consider the ideal negotiation to be what you call a win-win situation, it's an opportunity to explore together ways to change your own belief system about who you are and what your reality is about, in such a manner that you're also helping the other people in the negotiation do the same thing for themselves, so that everyone becomes more of who they are and can experience the reflection of that discovery. But it's not about getting that thing, getting that result, achieving that goal, getting what you want. It's about having an experience called a negotiation. It doesn't lead to something else. It is its own thing. And it's an opportunity to change your own beliefs so that you then become the vibration that will automatically attract whatever you have become the vibration of."

"So actions that you take, behaviors that you exhibit, are simply physicalized reflections — physicalized, shall we say, expressions of the vibrational state you happen to be in. The actions are not the idea of forcing something into being. That's the language of expectation. That's the language of goal orientation. That's the language of 'means to an end.'"

"When you understand everything — everything and every single moment is its own end, is its own experience with no connection to anything else — then you can stay in the moment with it, you can really appreciate the experience for what it is, and erase from your consciousness the idea that it's supposed to take you somewhere else. Because the most important thing is to stay completely present."

"We understand also again that because of your experience many times with physical reality and the belief systems that you have chosen to hold on to, that it may make the idea of remembering many of these principles seem to be difficult. But it really is not true. Understanding comes from the ability to remember simply more than one of these principles at the same time. So when we talk about, as we have for many years, the idea of stay present, stay in the moment; when we talk about the idea of drop your expectations; when we talk about the idea of taking action in the direction of your excitement without expectation; when we talk about the idea — and all these other ideas of what manifestation is — the idea is that when you go about thus then being who you say you prefer to be, the idea is to give yourself the ability to remember all of the principles at the same time, not just one, not just two. Then you have true understanding of the idea of the structure, of the nature of your existence."

"We understand that this can again, in your belief system, take a little practice, and that's all right. You're getting better at it, because if you weren't, we wouldn't be having this conversation. But the idea is that you can begin to truly incorporate these principles of being in the moment more fully, more fully, so that when such ideas come up as, 'Isn't taking an action actually in a sense creating an expectation?' — you can answer the question yourself: 'No.' Because the idea of being in the moment is that there is no separation between action, between behavior, and the thing itself. You're simply reflecting the vibration that you are for its own sake, because that's who you are, not because the action will get you something somewhere down the road in the future — which doesn't exist, which is an illusion."

"The more you allow yourself to apply all these principles in the present and see everything, and define — most importantly — define everything as distinctly its own experience in the present for its own sake, for what it is, you get out of it and have no concern for anything else. Don't connect it to anything else. Then your life becomes an ecstatic explosion of synchronicity, where everything starts to fall into place. It doesn't mean again you take no action, but the actions are not getting you those things. The actions are simply, in a sense, a verification — a physical verification, a physical expression of the frequency that you prefer to be in. That's what it is."

And in fact, you're actually ignoring the very experience that you designed for yourself to learn by the process itself."

Every moment you're creating what you're experiencing. Don't ignore it. Don't set it aside. Don't shove it off. Don't send it to camp. Experience it and define it. Find the definition of the experience you're having that works in the present, and bring to bear all the principles at once that we have discussed, at the same time."

Everything is already here. Everything exists here and now. You don't have to pull it from the future way out there in the nether world, the foggy far future. It is here now."

Because the idea of any visualization you may have created to symbolize what you prefer — which again is fine as a technique, fine as a permission slip, as long as you don't bring the expectation in it, as long as you don't label it as a means to an end — the visualization can represent the idea of the reality that you are manifesting, that represents your true preferred self, your true preferred life, your true preferred reality. But the idea is that that reality already exists, and by trusting and allowing and having what you call faith and knowledge in the idea of whatever it is you're experiencing now as being a part of that experience — and not just a pathway to it, but a part of that experience — you will experience it more fully. And by experiencing what is going on right now for its own sake more fully, you will change your vibration to be that which is more capable of rendering visible the reality that is already here but invisible to you at the moment."

"So you don't have to go out there to get it in that sense. But the actions that you do, the actions that you take, the behaviors that you express — that are hand in hand with the vibrational state that you're in — because that's automatic: whatever vibrational state you're in, you automatically exhibit behaviors that are germane to that state. And thus then, when you do that, when you behave the state, you make it more and more and more probable for you to see the reality that is vibrationally representative of that state. That's what manifestation is. It's just removing blinders.
