**Once upon a time there was a person so afraid of life that they sealed themselves up in a deep dark cave. And they sealed the entrance to that cave so nothing could get out and nothing could get in. And there they dwelt in darkness for a long, long, long time.**

But one day, after they had spent countless days, countless months and countless years forgetting the outside world, forgetting that anything existed but the darkness of the cave, one day, one surprising day, a flicker of a memory of the outside world entered their mind. They did not know from where it came. They thought perhaps for a moment they were going mad. But then that flicker turned into a brighter and brighter and brighter memory that once upon a time there was something else, something more than the darkness of the cave, something beyond their little world, something Beyond themselves.

And as day by day by day this idea of another place and another time grew within them, grew From a spark to a burning bright light, they began to realize that there might be something beyond the walls of the cave. But still they were afraid to unseal it and find out, for they were afraid of the unknown outside that entrance, afraid there would be nothing, afraid there would be Annihilation, afraid, afraid, afraid.

And the fear that they generated even as the light grew brighter began to split off little fearful clumps bouncing around beside them in the cave, little tiny beings running around in fear and confusion. And these little beings realizing that now they were trapped in this cave with this person sought any way, any crack, any crevice by which they could Escape. But they found none. And so they grew and they grew and they multiplied and began to fill the cave with more and more and more fearful little beings yelling and running and crying and sobbing and screaming, pleading to escape. And one day the person wandered to the entrance to make sure it was sealed tight and they thought they heard a small sound. And they put their ear to the seal and they could hear very faintly something knocking, trying to get in. And they backed away experiencing even more fear and they secured the entrance to the cave to make sure nothing from the outside would ever come in, for they greatly feared what that would be and what they would find. And they greatly feared that it would come in and it would consume them and they would be destroyed.

But the little ones in the cave that were with them kept crying "Let us out! Let us out! For we do not belong here with you. We must seek where we fit. We must seek Where We Belong."

"No," said the person. "No. I am too afraid, too afraid to open that door for if you rush out something else will rush in and it will consume me and annihilate me and I will be no more."

"No, no, no," said the little ones. "There is something Beyond. If it wants in, it must belong here as we belong elsewhere."

"No, no, no," said the person and kept them within. And so bit by bit and day by day the little ones that were split off from its fear in the darkness grew and grew and grew until they filled the cave. And as they filled the cave and became more desperate to leave, they began banging against the cave wall, against the Seal of rock that had been placed there by the person. But still they refused to open the seal.

And day by day the noise became greater and day by day the pounding became greater, but both from within and without, as those within tried desperately to escape and those without tried desperately to come in. And bit by bit as the numbers grew both within and without, they pounded against that seal and day by day the pounding began to Chip Away, wear away at that seal. Bit by bit, day by day, more and more that seal grew thinner and thinner and thinner and thinner. More and more grew the pile of rubble, the pile of dust on the inside and outside of the Seal of the cave.

And bit by bit did the person inside begin to realize that it would be inevitable that these would escape and something would come in. And they found no other way, no way to strengthen that seal.
