### Mechanics

All the things that we are discussing are not philosophies. We are talking mechanics. We are talking physics. We are talking action and reaction, cause and effect. 

If you apply certain ideas in your life, the principles by which you have always created your reality—whether you knew it or not—you will get the result that you desire. You have created out of your own fears and out of your own convenience—and I do not mean this derogatorily—the concept that you call an unconscious mind. No one really has an unconscious mind. You are one unbroken, homogeneous consciousness, one single idea. The concept that you call an unconscious mind has become a convenient compartment into which you can place things that you have been taught to be afraid to look at about yourselves. But there is absolutely nothing about yourselves that you could ever discover that cannot be applied in a positive way, if you so say. Therefore, one of the understandings that you can now begin to have, and perhaps will grow to understand through this interaction today, is that much of the reality that you experience is the product of what you believe it to be on an unconscious level. In other words, your strongest fears, your strongest emotions, your strongest definitions, your strongest beliefs are what create your physical experiential reality; are what are the reasons that attract certain situations and opportunities to you. If you are creating your reality mostly unconsciously, then you are out of touch as to why certain things keep happening in your life. But if you begin to understand that you can create your reality consciously, if you can be consciously aware of the definitions you may have been brought up with that have created the reality you've gotten so far, and you can learn how to change those definitions in the way that you prefer to, then you can get the reality you prefer. And it is not difficult at all, I promise. If you find that it becomes difficult, it is the result of other belief systems that you have bought into that say that life must be difficult.
