## On Channeling and Past Lives

Many people who channel physically have, when they are in contact as you say with other consciousness, have for the most part made an agreement to do so before this physical life. 

That is not to say you cannot make that agreement if it is necessary for you during your physical life. But it is rarer in your terms. You will find that the understanding of connection will have been accomplished. The agreement will have been made when in your non-physical form so that your sense of timing would be very clear and understood. Do you follow me?

**Questioner:** I was contacted yesterday by Michelangelo who I worked with in the past life, helped a great deal.

**Bashar:** Only partially is that in a sense in your terms too.

You have within your own higher consciousness all of those abilities which when in your thoughts translated backwards into physicality are represented to you by that physical symbol. 

That individual does carry a great many physical individuals under his wing.

He allows his energy as a guide to touch many individuals upon your world. You have the ability to tap into that idea.

However, realize that in the process, it is for the purpose of allowing you not to rely on another consciousness, but to recognize those abilities within your own. Any entity in spirit form will never, ever, ever live your life, answer your questions for you. They will always be very willing to let you know that you have the exact same abilities and you may identify with them in that way. 

But they will always wish that you view yourself as an equal companion, as an equal creator. You will taste the flavor of their consciousness. You may recognize the flavor and atmosphere of their personality within their wishes for you. But always, always, always allow yourself to recognize when it is also your own abilities coming forth with those fountains of information.

**Questioner:** I'd like to know more about channeling from a personal point of view. Channeling directly into beings such as yourself that can on a daily basis answer questions.

**Bashar:** Realize you can, as you say, on a daily basis answer your own questions. First of all, one of the ideas you understand as channeling is that you may at any time you wish always channel your higher consciousness. It is always open for you. There is not always a need in your terms for every individual to be as you say in communication with consciousness.

### The Dream Paradox

**Questioner:** I don't understand the dreamlike state

**Bashar:** You may think of it as a parallel existence which is the blueprint for your physical existence. 

Really, right now you are far more asleep than when you are dreaming. 

This is a focused dream with in your terms self-imposed blinders. 

You are living one dream. When you are in the dream reality, you are experiencing all dreams. 

Therefore, you are in a sense perceptually far more aware and so in your terms far more awake.

**Questioner:** So, we mocked up this idea of being asleep for a particular reason that we're going through. 

Yes. So, here we are sleeping, wanting to be awake, and then we're awake wanting to sleep. 

**Questioner:** I don't understand that.

**Bashar:** Because you are trying to make too much of it. 

Simply realize once again that part of what you are experiencing as a problem of expression is simply that when you chose to exist in physical reality by choosing certain ideas of limitation, of restriction in order to express and experience another path of becoming more self-aware, you included within that path that inability to relate to that idea. 

You have limited yourself on purpose, away from being able to understand both sides at the same time. 

This paradox is very, very, very livable in a non-physical universe. 

But in a physical universe, it is not. 

And so it seems confusing. That is partly the purpose: to experience the idea of two sides which cannot coexist. 

Yet you know they must. This spurs you on to discover more about your own awareness. 

When you allow paradox to exist within your life as you can, then every time you experience the paradox, you will know it will be a clue that you are dealing more with your higher consciousness. 

There's simply one more creative way of creating. Allow yourself to simply live.
