## Limitations

Your society is dissolving limitations, going beyond your limitations, expanding your notions of your consciousness.

In this way, you are not at the mercy of some of the negative limitations that you have created and set up in your society. 

Sometimes you think that spirituality means you have to go beyond all the limitations to dissolve every single one of them and detach yourself from physical reality completely.

This is either literally by evolving beyond it in order to be a perfected spiritual being, or aesthetically by detaching yourself from the material things of society in order to show that you are very spiritual and are not seduced by the mundane attributes of everyday life.

But Limitation has many positive effects.

1. Physical reality

This cannot happen without some self-imposed limitation on your consciousness.

Physical reality is a discrete experiential dimension, and by not imposing certain kinds of restrictions on your consciousness, you would not be capable of focusing down to the degree that you have in order to have what you call a physical experience. Because your natural state is an unlimited, totally expansive consciousness. 

So in order to crystallize yourself, shall we say, to materialize your consciousness in physical form, you do have to impose some set of limitations upon yourself in order to have that kind of reality experience.

2. Time Lag

in non-physical reality, then you are in a sense a thought form, a thought form living in a world of thought form.

There is no concept of real space and time on that level. In the non-physical world, you are at one with the reality there; there is no segregation, no separation in these concepts. 

And as you think, as you conceive of a thing, it instantly manifests and you have the experience.

on some levels, many of you say, "Well, this sounds pretty good, I want to have a manifestation as soon as I think of it." 

But recognize something: you are beings of polarity, dark and light, and on some of those levels, it is still possible for you to experience many negative aspects of your total consciousness.

if you have a thought in that way of a negative thing, it instantly manifests and you have to deal with it instantaneously.

And so creation was clever enough to have a reality wherein you have time lag, time and space volume, some degree of segregation and separation, that gives you, shall we say, a breather, a vacation from your instantaneous manifestation, so that you do not necessarily always have to experience every little thing of yourself immediately. 

That you can ponder and wonder and investigate and explore and add rich diversity to the understanding of the processes involved of the creation of yourselves, rather than having to instantly deal with every little thought that you have. So in that context, physical reality is very beneficial in giving you an opportunity to view the creation of yourselves in a time frame in which you can take your time, in which you can see things from a different perspective, rather than simply immediately being overwhelmed by every little thought, every little idea. And in that context, physical reality and the limitations that are inherent in it are very beneficial.

So do not be too eager to run away from all of your limitations. You can learn much in a very unique manner by letting yourself use the limitations creatively, using them in the same way that you use paint for a picture—to choose and pick, to understand, to explore deeply all the nuances and meanings of the meandering that you do in the myriad of ways that you have of looking at things and all the different perspectives and all the subtle details.

All the time that may seem to take—the more you enjoy that journey you have created, the richer the journey will be. And you will not have to be so impatient to get through all these details. The more you use the details, the more you learn what they have to inform you of, what they have to offer you, then paradoxically, the more accelerated you will become. Because you'll be having so much fun on every little detail that comes up, you'll not be paying attention to the idea that something better has to come along. You'll be living in the moment, and that's how things accelerate.

But again, paradoxically, you will be truly fully using and savoring every little limiting detail because it always comes like a seed with the potential for a great amount of blossoming information that can really enlighten you as to what kind of Creator being you really are. For do remember, as many of your ancient texts do say, you are made in the image of creation. You are a Creator being. You are a self-reflective, self-aware Creator Consciousness. 

Every little thing that you experience is totally, absolutely, and completely significant. There is no wasted time, there are no insignificant details. 

It is all a marvelous orchestration and choreography of Creatorhood. Cherish it, savor it. While you have created it, it will all be capable of adding to the overall knowledge of yourselves and enriching the concept of what you are as spirits and as physical beings. Because really, in that context, there is no difference. The greatest act, the greatest spiritual act that you can possibly have, is to live your physical lives as fully and joyfully and creatively as you possibly can as long as you are physical. That is the greatest act of spirituality that you can accomplish.

So in light of this notion, do not be so eager to run away from the things that you don't enjoy. Jump in, dive in, explore why there are those fears, those pains, those doubts. They all have information to tell you about yourself, to discover about yourself, so that you can become, by integrating that information, the expanded being you were created to be. Do not fear your fear. Fear is only there to tell you something you don't yet know about yourself. The only way it becomes unmanageable is if you ignore it, if you deny it, if you suppress it. If you don't own something, you haven't the power in your mind to change it. But if you own the pain, you own the fear, you know that there are beliefs and definitions of life within you that are responsible for your attraction to yourself and interpretation of situations as painful, as fearful.

Then get in touch with those beliefs, with those definitions, and in so doing can transform those definitions into the ones you prefer to be representative of your reality. And in so transforming them, you transform the physical reality.

Because physical reality is only the shadow of your soul. It is you. It is an extension of your consciousness. And it is not really outside you, and you are not really in it. It's in you.

So remember, you are creation. It is not that you were created and placed in creation. You are the Creator's creation.

When you act in accordance to that knowledge, to that frequency of unconditional love starting with yourselves, you experience the reality of unconditional love in your physiological lives.

Only you make those segregations. So there are those limitations that do need to be dissolved. At the same time, use the limitations that work for you to paradoxically accelerate yourself into a state of balance so that you love yourself as infinitely and unconditionally as creation does.

And in so doing, automatically operate on a frequency where it is easy and effortless and automatic to spread unconditional love among yourself as well. This is our suggestion. You don't have to do it. It's your planet after all.
