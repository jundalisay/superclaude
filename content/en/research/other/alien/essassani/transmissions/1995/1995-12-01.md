### Introduction: The Triad of Manifestation

We would like to reinforce the concept of intention as it must be:
- aligned with action and
- married to lack of expectation

This is the triad, the mechanism for:
- remaining in balance
- remaining in your center
- remaining in the present
- living in the now

 At the same time, it makes yourself most magnetically attractive to the manifestation of physical reality that will be most highly reflective of your joy.

Intention that you have an idea of who and what ideally you know yourself to be. Action that you will take your intention and actualize it through physiological action to show through that action that you are willing to be open to the attraction. 

It grounds the intention. It grounds the idea. Locks it in your physiological reality. Your actions are the underscoring of your intention. You can intend something all day long. If you're not willing to show by your actions that you are aligned with that idea, it will remain in the realm of the aeric.

And then, as we have outlined from time to time, you must go forward in these actions with the full intention that what you imagine to be true for you is true.

But you must have absolutely zero expectation that it has to manifest exactly as you originally thought.

This balancing act is what allows you to remain open to the unexpected surprises and expansiveness of the infinite.

For in most cases, as we have said, when you insist that the manifestation must occur exactly as you have imagined it, you are actually shortchanging yourself and not allowing the manifestation to come about in a way that might even be far more expanded than you were capable of imagining.

So allow yourself to have a healthy dose of the unexpected.

Let the unknown be your friend.

So that in having full intention and acting in accordance with that intention—being your true self, acting on your excitement—by having no expectation that it must come out that way, you will paradoxically be allowing it the best opportunity to come out perhaps not only the way you did originally imagine it in its best possible format.

But you will be allowing it to come out perhaps even better than you imagined it, by removing those structures of expectation and insistence that you have been taught by your society to utilize.



So keep it simple for the reality is complex but the mechanism of its creation is simple. Intention action no expectation will keep you firmly in the now in the present which is where you need to be to receive anything that you could possibly receive anyway. If you're not in the now, if no one is home, when opportunity knocks, you can't be there until you see it. You will not see it. It'll be invisible to you.
