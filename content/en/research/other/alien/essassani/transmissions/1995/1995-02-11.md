## The Concept of Unity and Individuality

Unity and Oneness is the product of strengthening your individuality rather than simply blending with the masses and becoming homogeneously indistinct.

Each and every one of you is unique for a reason because each and every being is a:
- unique perspective
- unique experience of the infinite

Everyone is all the different ways that the infinite has of experiencing itself, and therefore the uniqueness is to be cherished, not lost in the creation of unity.

As we have described in the analogy many times, if you look at your world as a gigantic puzzle picture and each and every one of you as a piece of the puzzle, you will see very quickly that in order to create the true whole picture, each and every piece with its own unique shape has a place to fit and must retain that shape in order to fill the whole it was designed to fill.

In order to create the whole picture, if that piece attempts to change its shape and not be what it is, then it doesn't fit in the whole and the whole picture suffers as a result.

So retaining and amplifying your unique individuality, being the fullest person that you can be, is fundamentally the fulfillment of the purpose of your life and allows the best possible opportunity for the whole reality to become more cohesive, more integrated, to act in a more holistic manner. Because every time each piece is the fullest piece it can be, it acts as a reflection, a living example for all other components that they can also be the fullest piece they can be.

Of course, holographically speaking, every single piece contains all the information of the whole, but from a unique perspective. Saying that you are a piece of the whole is not in any way meant to diminish the idea that you contain, each and every one of you, the total sum of All-That-Is, but expressed through a particular point of view, a particular facet of the multi-dimensional crystal.

You have heard a lot of talk of the concept that everyone is one, all is one, and this is so. But more often than not you hear this concept presented to you in what might be called a classically spiritual or non-physiological context. We would like at this time to stress the idea that Oneness is also experienced on the physiological level, as well as the spiritual, astral, psychic level that you are all now beginning to explore.
