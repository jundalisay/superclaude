**Caller:** What is the difference between positive and negative beliefs?

**Caller:** Are emotions positive or negative?

**Bashar:** Emotions are reflections of beliefs. Generally, high vibrational states are associated with positive/integrative energy, and low vibrational states with negative/separating energy. However, you must discern the source. For example, anger can be a rush of recognition of misalignment (positive/useful) if it lasts only briefly. If it persists, it becomes a negative judgment. Death is an invitation to stretch your consciousness and expand the relationship. You are never actually disconnected; physical reality is just an idea that a spirit has. When you pass, you awaken to the fact that you never left spirit.

**Caller:** How do we handle regret and rumination?

Acknowledge the emotion, ask what belief would have to be true to generate it, and decide if you want to hold that belief. Take a moment of pause when reacting to bring unconscious beliefs into conscious awareness.
