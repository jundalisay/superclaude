### The Game of Cybo


In our ancient language, the word Cybo is the number 13 and also stands for transformation. 

When our society was first created, our hybrid civilization was placed on as Essassani.

We were given many tools and experiences. Opportunities were designed for us so that our civilization would grow in very certain ways.

One of the things was the creation of certain games that we would play to lead us down certain avenues of experience. 

One of those games is what we are now relating to you in Las Vegas as Cybo, which stands for 13 in transformation.

The game aims to expand your synchronicity.

As you play more and more, you start experiencing more and more synchronicity in the game. That eventually can begin to leak into your life by training you to get into that state of being that experiences accelerated synchronicity.

The game is played from one to six individuals. 

You can actually do it by yourself as a permission slip exercise to expand your own synchronicity, or you can have the fun of playing with other individuals. 

It can be more, but the way the game is structured usually is done with six at the most, but that's up to you.

As we are from the future, you could say that this game is a 10,000-year-old game from the future.

It is played with a 12-sided die --- a die with 12 sides and 12 numbers on it.

There is a grid of those 12 numbers arranged in a very specific way. 

There are 13 rounds. You go around and around with each player one round at a time, 13 times.

In each round, you are given 3 rolls in an attempt to get 3 numbers in a row—not necessarily in order, although that's another level. 

Each number is represented by 1 point. If you get 3 in the same row in any order, you then earn 3 points. 

If you get the 3 numbers in any particular row—whether straight across, up or down in a column, or even diagonal—you still get those 3 points in any order.

But if you get the numbers in the actual sequential order that they are arranged in the row, then you get nine points. And this is the beginning of the synchronicity.

There are different levels to this game. 

There is a beginning, advanced, a Master Level, and a Grandmaster level. 

The beginning level allows you to have 4 rolls in order to get the 3 numbers. 

The advanced level is just 3 rolls to get the 3 numbers. 

The master level is you getting 3 numbers in a row in order. Then you actually repeat that somewhere in the 13 rounds.

<!-- , you have entered the master level.  -->

At that point, your synchronicity is so strong that you can actually begin to roll the same numbers over and over and over again.

A Grandmaster can in all 13 rounds roll the same 3 numbers in the same order for every round. 

When your synchronicity is that high, nothing seems beyond you.
