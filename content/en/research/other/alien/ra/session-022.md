## Private Property and Money Reduced Lifespan

{{< r a="Don Elkins" >}}
<sup>5</sup> Was this drastic drop from 700-year life span to less than 100 years during this second 25,000-year period from an intensification of selfishness?{{< /r >}}
<!-- a… of a condition of lack of service to others? Is this correct? -->

{{< l a="Ra" >}}
This is partly correct. 

By the end of the 2nd cycle, the Law of Responsibility had begun to be effectuated by the increasing ability of entities to grasp those lessons  to be learned in this density.

Thus, entities had discovered many ways to indicate a bellicose nature, not only as tribes or nations, but in personal relationships, each with the other.

This led to:
- **the concept of barter giving way to the concept of money**
- **the concept of ownership having won ascendancy over the concept of non-ownership on an individual or group basis.**

Each entity then was offered many more subtle ways to manipulate others masked as either:
- service towards others or
- service to self. 

As each lesson was understood, those lessons of sharing, of giving, of receiving in free gratitude could be rejected in practice.

Without demonstrating the fruits of such learn/teaching, the lifespan became greatly reduced, for the ways of honor/duty were not being accepted.

It suggests that an entity not receive more experience in more intensity than it may bear. [shorten the selfishness]

Thus, the shortened life span is due to the necessity for removing an entity from the intensity of experience from the rejection of wisdom and love.

This expereince is then reflected back into the Creator's consciousness without being accepted as part of the self.

This then [bounces back to the entity] causing the entity to need healing and evaluation of the incarnation.
