Sunday, 27th June 1976, 01:47 hrs

{{< l a="Quetzal">}}
Unfortunately, I have to explain to you that a demonstration for the purpose of filming and photographing cannot take place today. As I noticed, human beings are everywhere on the roads and at events, observing the sky very intensely regarding various fireworks related to the events. I have also just noticed that there is an automobile at the crossroads in front of Hinwil in which two people from the police force are sitting and are vigilantly inspecting the surroundings.
{{< /l >}}

{{< l a="Semjase">}}
Unfortunately, it is not possible to change it, so we can only take care of the issues concerning Amata.
{{< /l >}}

{{< r a="Billy">}}
You once told me that you were going to put some contraceptives in your food to keep the birth rate under control. The last time you made contact, however, you said completely different things.
{{< /r >}}

{{< l a="Semjase">}}
Then you asked the questions very imprecisely. It is true that we add natural herbal contraceptives to our food. However, this only refers to the fact that this form of contraception is used when there is an excess birth rate, i.e. when overpopulation problems occur.

This method is usually used when a race has reached the point where it recognises that a planet can only carry and feed a certain number of human life-forms without artificial intervention. So this is related to the evolution of mind and consciousness.

Otherwise, however, it is also the case that all human life-forms in our worlds can voluntarily obtain the foods containing contraceptives in order to be able to regulate the number of births. Not everyone does, however, because human beings prefer to use other methods of contraception, although the active ingredients are not harmful in any way, which unfortunately cannot always be said of your contraceptives.

In general, the ingesting of these foods interspersed with active substances does not therefore occur, so everything can be taken purely voluntarily.

A general mandatory taking of birth control has to be reintroduced in order to bring about a normal birth rate adapted to the respective world.
{{< /l >}}

{{< r a="Billy">}}
Oh, that is how it is. Then the next question: Did you find out anything about my colleague Köbi?
{{< /r >}}

{{< l a="Quetzal">}}
It has been found that his behaviour is due to his own psychological-negative influence. This has no connection whatsoever with any external negative influences. As I have analysed, he has a profoundly pathological and very unreasonable attitude towards his professional activity and the environment. It would be better for him if he would balance himself in this respect and integrate himself into your own manner of discipline. Explain to him that even we cannot avoid doing our daily work, albeit not to the extent that is customary with you. On our home worlds all people work their daily workload, but so do we, who have our tasks here on the Earth, although our hours of employment are much higher than our home worlds and your working hours. Our hours of occupation are usually 16 of the earthly hours per day, often even more.
{{< /l >}}

{{< r a="Billy">}}
Köbi will be able to read your statement. Hopefully he will see clearly what is requested of him. By the way, I am supposed to convey a warm greeting from everyone to both of you.
{{< /r >}}

{{< l a="Semjase">}}
That pleases me very much, also I convey all my love and my greetings.
{{< /l >}}

{{< l a="Quetzal">}}
I am also very pleased about it, and I also wish to convey my love and greetings.
{{< /l >}}

{{< r a="Billy">}}
I will be very happy to tell them. By the way, Semjase, I have a surprise for you: You said you wanted to get yourself a mechanical writing instrument, a typewriter, did you not?
{{< /r >}}

{{< l a="Semjase">}}
Sure, Quetzal is in agreement with that and he will endeavour to obtain the necessary money.
{{< /l >}}

{{< r a="Billy">}}
Excellent, but that is not necessary. You know, our friend Olga is so excited about your plan that she provides you with a typewriter.
{{< /r >}}

{{< l a="Semjase">}}
She cannot do that, because she must also have to buy such a device first.
{{< /l >}}

{{< r a="Billy">}}
Now you are thinking illogically, because Olga really likes to do that and all four women are incredibly grateful to you and almost crazy with joy to offer it to you.
{{< /r >}}

{{< l a="Semjase">}}
You are exaggerating.
{{< /l >}}

{{< r a="Billy">}}
Of course, but how else can I explain it to you? Anyway, Olga already has such a mechanical instrument in her possession, and that is exactly what she wants to give you. She already brought it with her, but unfortunately I could not bring it because I have forgotten it.
{{< /r >}}

{{< l a="Semjase">}}
Olga's decision moves me very much. I give her my deepest thanks.
{{< /l >}}

{{< r a="Billy">}}
She will be happy to hear it.
{{< /r >}}

{{< l a="Semjase">}}
The joy is all mine. At the beginning of the week, I will call you and ask you to bring me the writing instrument. I will also bring my sister Pleija, because she wants to get to know you and take a look at your barbaric means of transportation.
{{< /l >}}

{{< r a="Billy">}}
Great, I will be happy to teach her how to drive this dangerous vehicle. How do you feel about that?
{{< /r >}}

{{< l a="Semjase">}}
Do you not dare.
{{< /l >}}

{{< r a="Billy">}}
You will see.
{{< /r >}}

{{< l a="Semjase">}}
Pleija will be happy about it, because she is very interested in these things, even if it seems barbaric to me.
{{< /l >}}

{{< r a="Billy">}}
I told Semjase last time that we earth-crawlers are barbarians, and I am quite happy with that.
{{< /r >}}

{{< l a="Semjase">}}
Sure, I am aware of that, it is partly even good that way.
{{< /l >}}

{{< r a="Billy">}}
Finally, you react. Barbarians do not live all wrong, do they?
{{< /r >}}

{{< l a="Quetzal">}}
That is probably so, it has its advantages, because the human being is more resistant and capable of enormous achievements.
{{< /l >}}

{{< r a="Billy">}}
Thanks for the flowers, I am happy about it like a Reed Sparrow.
{{< /r >}}

{{< l a="Semjase">}}
I have to leave now, because I still have a job to do in the mountains you call Jura. Goodbye, and see you soon.
{{< /l >}}

{{< r a="Billy">}}
Goodbye, dear girl, do not strain yourself too much, and when do you actually sleep?
{{< /r >}}

{{< l a="Semjase">}}
I suppose that question rests in reciprocity, does it not?
{{< /l >}}

{{< r a="Billy">}}
Maybe.
{{< /r >}}

{{< l a="Quetzal">}}
You should also go now and bring Amata here. I will show you to a place with her. Here, take this. Let her chew it and eat it. Then you will come to me. I will be expecting you again at 3:30 hrs.
{{< /l >}}

{{< l a="Semjase">}}
Be ready at 16:00 hrs. Pleija and I will wait for you at this time in a place west of yesterday's place of contact. Go to the southwest side of the forest where we will expect you.
{{< /l >}}
