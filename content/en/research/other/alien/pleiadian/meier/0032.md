Monday, 8th September 1975, 14:16 hrs

{{< l a="Semjase">}}
I have been receiving your call very strongly since yesterday.  
Are you already so far advanced with your work, that you are able to write down the new report?
{{< /l >}}

{{< r a="Billy Meier">}}
Not yet, but something has gotten in my way, and now I do not know what to do.
{{< /r >}}

{{< l a="Semjase">}}
Is this so important then, that you must call me?
{{< /l >}}

{{< r a="Billy Meier">}}
I think so, because as you know, I am currently writing down all the contact reports which I had written in 1964 with Asket's recollection help. But now I cannot find the remaining reports. Apparently I have hidden them too well, or I simply lost them. I really do not know. What should I do?
{{< /r >}}

{{< l a="Semjase">}}
Can you really not find them?
{{< /l >}}

{{< r a="Billy Meier">}}
I would not have called you so urgently if I could.
{{< /r >}}

{{< l a="Semjase">}}
Sure, that would not be your style.  
Then let these reports be for the time being, because if you cannot find them, you probably do not have them anymore, but have you really checked everything thoroughly?
{{< /l >}}

{{< r a="Billy Meier">}}
Of course, I have really turned everything upside down.
{{< /r >}}

{{< l a="Semjase">}}
Then you must have lost them.
{{< /l >}}

{{< r a="Billy Meier">}}
I think so too; what should I do now?
{{< /r >}}

{{< l a="Semjase">}}
If you really cannot find them anymore, then it is not so important.  
I will get in touch with Asket and tell her about your mishap.  
She will perhaps transmit all the missing reports to you again.  
All the knowledge of the event and the conversations are indeed recorded.  
You can write down these reports once again at a later date.  
How far have you progressed?
{{< /l >}}

{{< r a="Billy Meier">}}
I was still able to write down the first conversation with Jmmanuel, but the other reports are missing.
{{< /r >}}

{{< l a="Semjase">}}
Good, then you have made a rather good progress.  
Leave an explanation at the conclusion of the first contact with Jmmanuel, that you cannot find the remaining reports.
{{< /l >}}

{{< r a="Billy Meier">}}
I have already done that, and I am putting this contact with you now as a direct sequel.
{{< /r >}}

{{< l a="Semjase">}}
You really think of everything. –  
So we want to address the remaining like this:  
I transmit to you the second half of the report of your trip with me to Asket.  
Make a corresponding note and then continue with these reports.  
We will simply weave in the missing Asket reports later, when Asket transmits them to you.  
Should that not be the case, then it is not so important, because to my knowledge the further conversations with Jmmanuel deal with things which refer to his history etc., as well as to facts which anyway, with some exceptions, are not very important for the Earth-humans to know.
{{< /l >}}

{{< r a="Billy Meier">}}
Good, then you may begin at once.
{{< /r >}}

{{< l a="Semjase">}}
Steady, steady, it cannot go that fast.  
I was not prepared for this situation and cannot free myself for these things yet.  
I can only do it starting tomorrow, because everything must be prepared first.  
I still have a lot of other work to do and in addition, I am also currently undertaking another important task.
{{< /l >}}
