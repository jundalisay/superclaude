Tuesday, 15th July 1975, 09:46 hrs

{{< l a="Semjase">}}
On several occasions I have received your thoughts, but unfortunately I had to ignore them because my present task is very demanding for me.
And if I now contact you today, then for very specific reasons and only for a short time, because you are to prepare yourself for the early morning hours of the 17th of July, in two days therefore.

At the appropriate time I will then call you and pick you up at a suitable place.
Make sure you have enough footage, because this will be important.
You are the first human being on Earth to photograph a process which is of utmost importance and relevance for your race.
This is the coupling manoeuvre of the two American and Russian space capsules, which you are supposed to capture on film.
In this respect, we have created a special device that allows you to take better pictures with your camera – at least that's what we hope.
These will be the first photographs of this kind to show such a manoeuvre independently of the terrestrial space capsules.
Continue to prepare yourself to be absent for longer hours, because before and after the photo undertaking you will undertake a very long flight, which is still impossible even for many space travelling life-forms.
There are currently very few life-forms in the universe that are able to travel such long distances, because this is only possible through the refraction and use of hyperspace.
But where this was made possible by highly developed technologies, the universe no longer offers any limits.
Even space and time no longer form barriers, nor do the barriers between different universes.
As you know from earlier times, time-travel is also known to certain life-forms …

I also know Asket well.

But please do not interrupt me, because my contact time is very short:
We will not be able to make the next voyage in my small beam ship, because it is not suitable for such distances.
After finishing your photographic work, we will change to a very spacious ship.
So try to have a total of at least thirty hours, because with this ship we will really travel very far outside of all constellations and galaxies known to you and will measure several billion light-years.
You will also have the opportunity to take a few pictures of star systems and galaxies, etc., and also to see from a distance the original homeland of the human races.
Today it offers a strange picture, whose origin is based on a violent destruction of a very large star.
The destruction was triggered a very long time ago by a barbaric and power-hungry JHWH, which ended in the destruction of himself.
According to his senses and predictions, however, the destruction developed exactly in the form of the desired image, which was to serve the life-forms of the universe as a memorial to his power.
The predicted form was a gigantic image of an eye now looking through the universe as an evil memorial to human insanity and human greed for power, but otherwise millions in the entire universe, but of natural origin.
True to its eye-like appearance, the structure has been named JHWHMATA in connection with the producer since its creation.
This name means translated into your language: 'eye of God' or 'God's eye'.
According to your astronomical data and designations you will find the 'eye of God' in the constellation of Lyra, and you name it the Ring Nebula or M57.
These are the things I have to tell you today.
If you still have a question, then I still have a short time to answer it for you.
{{< /l >}}

{{< r a="Billy Meier">}}
I feel like I have been transferred to the realm of fantasy, Semjase. But tell me, where in heaven can I find the 'eye of God'?
{{< /r >}}

{{< l a="Semjase">}}
You will need a bigger telescope.
{{< /l >}}

{{< r a="Billy Meier">}}
Too bad. – With regard to the forthcoming journey, I have another question: If I calculate the distance in kilometres, what number does that produce?
{{< /r >}}

{{< l a="Semjase">}}
It surpasses all your mathematical calculation possibilities many times over.
It would therefore be absurd to mention numbers that I could only use my own terms.
But be aware that nobody on Earth has ever travelled such a long distance before you and will not do so in the next 2,000 years, because this journey will lead you to a barrier of this Universe.
{{< /l >}}

{{< r a="Billy Meier">}}
I am going crazy – you mean we are rushing to a place where our Universe touches another?
{{< /r >}}

{{< l a="Semjase">}}
Certainly.
{{< /l >}}

{{< r a="Billy Meier">}}
But then the few hours are not enough.
{{< /r >}}

{{< l a="Semjase">}}
You forget the paralysis of space and time through the use of hyperspace.
A hyper-jump over countless trillions and even higher nominal values of kilometres only takes fractions of a second.
{{< /l >}}

{{< r a="Billy Meier">}}
Man oh man, then this will so quickly become a short journey to the end of the Universe – a journey into eternity so to speak.
{{< /r >}}

{{< l a="Semjase">}}
Your thoughts are wrong, because we do not reach the end of the Universe, because such an end does not exist.
We merely enter and pass through a barrier of this Universe.
{{< /l >}}

{{< r a="Billy Meier">}}
That's what I meant – I just expressed myself inappropriately. But how can we actually undertake this journey when you are so busy with the Americans and Russians because of their history?
{{< /r >}}

{{< l a="Semjase">}}
The further work will be continued by others as I leave because I have been given an additional task related to the great journey, as you say.
{{< /l >}}

{{< r a="Billy Meier">}}
Oh, so then you are not just flying to the Universe barrier because you just want to give me this trip?
{{< /r >}}

{{< l a="Semjase">}}
You have grasped it, because it is connected with a very specific task.
But now I cannot talk with you any longer, for time is pressing and I still have various things to do.
{{< /l >}}
