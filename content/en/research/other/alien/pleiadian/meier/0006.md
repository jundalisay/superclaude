A field that is a headache for me, yet a wide-ranging science that has not brought us cognitions revealing the ultimate secrets. Concerning this, I can only explain what is known to us and what we assume to be given.

On the whole, there are only five very important points that we know exactly, while two further points are not certain yet. These lead into the secrets of Creation, which is unapproachable for us too. Nevertheless, I do not want to withhold our assumptions from you.

The life from beginning to end is divided into seven main stages or main periods. These in turn are divided into seven sub-periods, resulting in its entirety.

The sub-stages or sub-periods can be compared to grades in school, except that these must not be calculated for one year, but for one human lifetime. This means that a very specific form and goal of development lie in a sub-stage.

Once all seven sub-stages are passed through—which can take hundreds of thousands of years—a main period is completed.

The spiritual life-form is completely genderless, because gender is without exception only characteristic of organic life to guarantee its reproduction. But organic life, like spiritual life, is subject to the law of rebirth to guarantee development in both forms. Thus, every human life-form has to live through seven distinct main periods and their seven associated sub-stages.

In location-dependent organic life-forms, the periodic effects of the seasons are determinative. In spirit-evolutive life-forms such as the human being, the periods are irregular and set individually; they do not fall under a distinct cycle like plants. A rebirth of the spirit-dependent human being after bodily death can reoccur after fractions of a second, or only after decades, centuries, millennia, or hundreds of thousands of years.

If a spirit-conditioned life is destroyed prematurely without reaching a relative evolutionary goal, it is to its disadvantage. It cannot fully pass through and complete its sub-period and must repeat or complete this period through an intermediate life during a normal life cycle to achieve the goal.

The 7 main stages each represent one full period with in turn their sevenfold subdivisions. This would be one semester. Therefore, in your sense this would be called a semester-life, during which seven different subjects have to be completed. I want to explain this to you schematically:

1. Primary life

primary development of the intellect and the consciousness; primary intellectual and consciousness-based thinking; primary intellect-based thinking; primary use of intellect and consciousness-power; primary intellectual actions; primary wilful thinking and acting; intellectual conduct of the life. Life-forms in these stages are designated by already rationality-endowed Wesen as mentally ill, as idiots etc., whose consciousness and intellect, however, are in reality simply not yet developed in knowledge-based terms.

2. Rationality-based life

primary development of rationality; effective realisation of the rationality and its use; primary recognition and acknowledgement of higher influences; belief in higher influences without having knowledge; belief in higher powers, deluded belief, fear of evil, veneration of what is good, and so on – germination time for religions etc. This is the present stage of the average Earth-human. Then, primary recognition of the real reality, stage of knowledge-based development, research, first spiritual cognitions and their use: spiritual healing, telepathy, etc.; and primary development of knowledge and essence of wisdom.

3. Intellect-based life

high-level development of the intellect, high technology, second use of spiritual power with first cognitions, primary procreation of life-forms – this is the present stage of the educated Earth-human, scientists, etc. Then, realisation and use of knowledge, truth and essence of wisdom, with slow reduction of belief-assumptions; first utilisation of knowledge and essence of wisdom; recognition and utilisation of the laws of nature, creation of hyper technology, second procreation of life-forms; natural use of knowledge and essence of wisdom in recognition of spiritual powers, further reduction of belief-assumptions; life in the knowledge of the essence of wisdom, truth and the logic – this is the present stage of some few borderline and humanities scholars; and primary recognition of the reality as real Absolutum.

4. Real life

clear knowledge about the reality as real Absolutum; recognition of the spiritual knowledge and the spiritual essence of wisdom; utilisation of the spiritual knowledge and the spiritual essence of wisdom; recognition of the reality of the Creation and her laws – this is your present stage, which is already tending toward the next one; then life according to the creational laws, clarification of the spirit and the intellect, recognition of the real task and power of the spirit, total reduction of all belief-assumptions; deliberate and directed utilisation of spiritual and consciousness-based powers; and procreation of first viable life-forms.

5. Creational life

procreation and direction of life-forms; creation of machine/apparatus-based viable life-forms; development of spiritual and consciousness-based power for the control of material and organic life-forms – this is the present stage of our races; then wilful control of the life and all its forms and species; stage of recognitions, recollections of past lives, etc.; essence of wisdom kingship, which is JHWH, the second to last highest might-knowledge; and recognition of the spiritual peace, the universal love, and the creational harmony.

6. Spiritual life

acknowledgment and realisation of the spiritual peace, the universal love and the creational harmony; life in purely spiritual forms; spiritual Creations; disembodiment of the spirit from organic bodies; first spiritual existence; final spiritual existence; and transition into the Creation.

7. Creation-life

twilight sleep over seven periods; awakening and beginning of creating in the Creation as Creation, during seven periods; creating of life-forms; creating of new spirit in the relative absolute fulfilment of the Creation; creation of spiritual greatness in the Creation; relative absolute fulfilment of the Creation in the Creation; and last attaining of highest absolutely full development of the seventh period. The twilight sleep of the Creation outlasts seven periods, and 311,040,000,000,000 years amount to one period. By the twilight sleep of the Creation, all life and the entire universe cease to exist. Only after her newly awakening does she begin to create everything anew. During her twilight sleep there is neither space nor time; there is only the nothing, since they lie asleep in the unending womb of the Creation and the null time. If there is no creating thought, then there is also no power, no time, and no space; there is only duration in the nameless nothing.
