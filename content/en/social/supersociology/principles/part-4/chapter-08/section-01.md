Thinker Philosophies are created by or favor the thinker class, also known as the intellectuals or the brahmins in Hinduism. 

In the Republc by Plato, the thinker class is called the Aristocrats, as philosophers.

These are people who live by ideas, theories, or knowledge.

Thinkers thrive after their society has been made peaceful by warriors. This allows the peace that is needed by thinkers to think. 


The main feeling of thinkers is the need to think about perceptions which leads to ideas which leads to beliefs. 

The different groups of beliefs leads to:
- philosophy which is a system of beliefs
- religion which is a system of beliefs based on limitation


This desire to share beliefs often lead to religion or education or the spread of certain philosophies. 

The lack of thinkers within the human species necessarily makes Thinker Philosophies the least common and the Worker Philosophies most common.
