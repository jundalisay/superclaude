Purchasing power is the quantity of goods and services that a single unit of money or token can buy. This represents the effort that it can command. 

There are other measures of command of work:

- Miltary force: Soldiers can force a village or prisoners to work
- A beautiful or handsome face: Men can be made to do things for a beautiful woman
- Illegal drugs: addicts will steal just to get money to buy drugs

But legally, money is the standard command of work. Previously, barter was valid. 

{{< q a="Adam Smith" c="Book 2, Chapter 2" >}}
But the wealth or revenue is equal only to the purchasing power of money. It is equal to the money's worth, not the money
{{< /q >}}
