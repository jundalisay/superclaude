## Impact of Wireless Energy Transmission: Nationalization of Energy

The future discovery of long range wireless energy transmission, together with sustainable nuclear fusion and antimatter power, will make power generation fully integrated.

Currently, the power system is not integrated.

The power company is different from the company making the electric vehicle. 

This is because the electric vehicle uses batteries that store the power generated by the powerplant. 

This allows the manufacturer of the powerplant and the electric vehicle to be different companies. 

But wireless transmission will use the relational frequency between:
- the aether around the Earth
- the aetherspace or atomic matter of the powerplant's Tesla coil
- the aetherspace or atomic matter of electric motor of the electric vehicle

And so the company that makes the powerplant also configures the electric vehicle.

This is true for households which will have its own power receiver that matches the coil of the powerplant. 

This is similar to the satellite internet from Starlink requiring satellite dishes also from Starlink.

Only governments can amass the capital needed to make such power generation (nuclear fusion), wireless transmission and wireless consumption systems. 

This is why the discovery of aether technologies will lead to the nationalization of certain industries.

Until that time comes, it would be wise to start semi-nationalizations for the sake of improving efficiencies and gainign the trust that will allow capital to be amassed for such expensive future developments.
