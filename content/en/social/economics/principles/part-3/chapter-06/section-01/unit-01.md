The Natural Balance Model puts supply demand and trade into balance with one another. 

Economics only focuses on supply and trade, but ignores demand and balance. 

This is because Economics focuses on the self which is satisfied with the self getting a lot of supply through trade.

It does not really care about the demand of other people, and the economic balance that keeps the supply spread properly to address all demand. 

Otherwise, there would be no poverty and crashes. 


## Demand Capital Industry Trade Balance

The Third Law focuses on economic balance which in Supereconomics is the balance between Demand Capital Industry and Trade.

We can do this because our perspective is the society organism which is the collective of all souls in a society. 

This collective includes "2nd density" beings such as plants, animals, and mineral resources which are mostly in the Demand model.
