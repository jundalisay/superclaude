Undercapitalization is a problem in organizations and businesses that reveals itself in the medium and long terms.

This is solved by long term planning, cooperation, and organizational stability.


A common undercapitalization is the lack of training of staff because they might use their learnings to apply for a job at a rival company where the cost of training was borne by their current company.
