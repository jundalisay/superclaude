Dharmic Productivity is the quantity and quality produced that is in line with one's dharma or personal interest or passion.
- This only applies to people, animals, and plants. 
- The plants aspect is evident in agriculture. 
- The animals aspect is evident in animal husbandry. 

This takes health into account, which includes mental health.
