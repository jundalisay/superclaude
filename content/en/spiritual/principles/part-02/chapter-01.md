We classify discipline as the 2nd path of spirituality as mirroring the warrior class and 2nd Rule of motion as effort. 

This is called Raja Yoga in Patanjali's system

## Raja Yoga

This is also called the “Royal Path" as it is the path of **order, discipline, and mastery**. 

It does not rely chiefly on emotion or scriptural study alone; instead, it lays out an eight‑limbed system—beginning with moral rules (*yamas*), daily observances (*niyamas*), physical and mental discipline, and meditation—designed to gradually quiet the mind, purify habits, and prepare the practitioner for union. 

The foundation is clear: **freedom comes through mastery, and mastery comes through discipline.**
