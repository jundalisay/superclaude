## The Path of Love

Bhakti Yoga is the **Yoga of Devotion and Love**. Its core truth is simple and powerful: the fastest, most natural way to unite with the Divine is through love. Not through dry intellect, not through rigid discipline alone, but by opening the heart, surrendering the ego, and loving the Beloved above all else. It does not ask you to reject the world—it asks you to see the Divine in all things, and to love the One with every part of your being.
