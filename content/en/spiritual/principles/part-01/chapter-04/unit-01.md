## The Law of Attraction: Intention in Motion

The Law of Attraction rests on an action foundation: **consciousness shapes experience, and clear desire is the starting point of creation**. 

It teaches that your thoughts, beliefs, and feelings broadcast a signal that draws matching experiences to you—but it also emphasizes that *you must act on what you desire*. Inspired action is not optional; it is how you cooperate with the universe to bring potential into form.

This mirrors Karma Yoga in three key ways:

- **Desire clarified and elevated**: Karma Yoga asks you to purify your desires and align them with what is true and good. The Law of Attraction teaches the same: focus on what you truly want—not what you fear—and raise your vibration to match it.
- **Action as partnership**: You do not simply wait and hope. You take steps, seize opportunities, and move toward your goal. In Karma Yoga this is *action as worship*; in the Law of Attraction it is *inspired action as co‑creation*.
- **Detachment from the “how”**: You act with purpose, but you do not obsess over exactly *how* results will arrive. You trust that your aligned effort, combined with universal order, will unfold the right outcome at the right time.


## The Shared Heart

Both teach that **you are a conscious creator, and your life is the fruit of your actions and intentions**. Karma Yoga says: act with love and surrender the result. The Law of Attraction says: intend clearly, act joyfully, and allow the universe to deliver. When you pursue your desires with focus, integrity, and openness—without grasping or forcing—your effort becomes a spiritual practice, and your life becomes the masterpiece you help create.
