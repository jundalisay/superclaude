| Group / Teacher | Core Focus | Approx. Combined Reach / Activity | Key Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Eckhart Tolle** | Presence, non-duality, awakening | **~8.3–10 million** | Most mainstream; global bestsellers, Oprah-endorsed, massive cross-platform reach. |
| **Abraham-Hicks** | Manifestation, universal well-being, asking–receiving | **~6–8 million** | Esther Hicks channeling; huge seminars, books, and social media presence. |
| **Pleiadians (Marciniak / Meier)** | Cosmic wisdom, human origins, starseeds, ascension | **~1–2 million** | *Bringers of the Dawn* is iconic; #Pleiadians has 50M+ TikTok views. |
| **Ra (Law of One)** | Densities, cosmic evolution, service to self/others | **~500k–900k** | Dense, academic material; highly influential in metaphysical circles. |
| **Ashtar Command / Ashtar Sheran** | Galactic Federation, planetary shift, first contact | **~500k–1 million** | Active since 1950s; large network of channelers and online groups. |
| **Bashar (Essassani)** | Frequency, alignment, joyful creation, extraterrestrial perspective | **~400k–700k** | Darryl Anka channeling; consistent new content, very active YouTube & TikTok. |
| **Edgar Cayce Readings** | Past lives, Atlantis, holistic healing, ancient history | **~300k–500k** | Legacy work; maintained by ARE; steady sales and study groups. |
| **Arcturians** | Higher-dimensional healing, lightbody, cosmic guidance | **~200k–400k** | Milanovich, Scranton, Miller; very popular in modern ET channeling spaces. |
| **Seth Material** | “You create your own reality”, multidimensional self | **~200k–350k** | Jane Roberts; foundational influence, no active modern channeler. |
| **Andromedans (Alex Collier)** | Cosmic history, disclosure, human sovereignty | **~150k–300k** | Strong following in UFO/disclosure communities; regular webinars. |
| **Cassiopaeans** | Multidimensional reality, cosmic history, alternative science | **~100k–200k** | Laura Knight-Jadczyk; deep, research-heavy material; loyal niche. |
| **Raëlism** | Elohim creators, science + spirituality, geniocracy | **~80k–100k claimed; ~40–60k scholarly estimate** | Most structured formal ET religion; global organization. |
| **Ground Crew / Sheldan Nidle** | Galactic Federation, photon belt, planetary activation | **~50k–150k** | Long-running; evolved from contact predictions to spiritual preparation. |
| **Ramtha** | Ascension, quantum consciousness, ancient wisdom | **~20k–50k** | JZ Knight; smaller, more closed/structured student body. |
| **Aetherius Society** | Cosmic Masters, energy work, planetary service | **~10k–20k** | Since 1955; international centers, stable but small membership. |
| **Unarius Academy of Science** | Interdimensional science, past-life healing | **~5k–15k** | Educational/study-focused; low-key long-running presence. |
| **Keylontic Science** | Energy mechanics, multidimensional physics, ascension grids | **~low thousands** | Highly technical, specialized framework; very niche community. |

---

### Quick Takeaways
- **Largest overall**: Eckhart Tolle > Abraham-Hicks > Pleiadians
- **Most active ET/channeling content**: Bashar > Pleiadians > Arcturians
- **Most influential legacy**: Ra / Law of One > Seth > Edgar Cayce
- **Most structured/organized**: Raëlism > Aetherius Society > Ramtha
- **Most “alien-focused” organized groups**: Raëlism, Aetherius Society, Unarius
- **Most active online now**: Pleiadians, Arcturians, Andromedan/Collier communities
- **Legacy influence**: Cayce still widely referenced even with no modern channeler
