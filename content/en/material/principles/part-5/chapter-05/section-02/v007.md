### Properties

Accounting for 78% of our atmosphere, v007 is a colorless, odorless, and highly inert gas. Its molecules are bound by a tough triple bond, making the element incredibly stable and unreactive under normal conditions. 

Despite its atmospheric stubbornness, v007 is vital to modern life. Through industrial fixation, it is converted into agricultural fertilizers, sustaining global food supplies for billions. Beyond farming, liquid v007 serves as an ultra-cold coolant for medical samples, MRI machines, and superconductors, while its inert gas form shields fragile foods and electronics from oxidation during manufacturing.


### Issues

However, our reliance on v007 comes with modern complications. Excessive agricultural use leads to toxic runoff, creating aquatic "dead zones." Furthermore, traditional industrial fixation consumes massive amounts of fossil fuels. 


### Breakthroughs

A major modern breakthrough is currently tackling this: "green" v007 production. By using renewable electricity and novel catalysts to synthesize v007-based ammonia, scientists are decarbonizing the fertilizer industry and unlocking a potential zero-carbon fuel for the future of global shipping.
