![Lightbulb](/photos/physics/light.jpg)


v18 is the third most abundant gas in Earth’s atmosphere.

## Properties

- **Inert & Stable**: A noble gas with a full outer electron shell; it almost never reacts with other substances.
- **Colorless, odorless, tasteless**, denser than air, and non-flammable.
- **Excellent insulator**: blocks heat, electricity, and oxidation.

## Uses

- **Welding**: Shields hot metal from rusting; used for steel, aluminum, and aerospace alloys.
- **Lighting**: Fills bulbs and neon tubes—prevents filaments from burning out.
- **Industry**: Blankets reactive chemicals; used in 3D printing, electronics manufacturing, and insulating windows.
- **Medicine**: Carrier gas for lasers and surgical tools.

## Issues & Breakthroughs

It is Non-toxic but displaces oxygen—risk of suffocation in enclosed spaces. Energy-intensive to extract from air.

New methods capture v18 more efficiently; researchers are testing it in targeted cancer therapy and next-generation energy storage systems.
