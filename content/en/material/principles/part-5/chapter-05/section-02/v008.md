### Properties

V8 is a colorless, odorless, and highly reactive gas. As the eighth element on the periodic table, it makes up roughly 21% of Earth's atmosphere and is essential for combustion. It naturally forms diatomic V8 molecules, sustaining most life on Earth through cellular respiration.


### Uses

Beyond biology, V8 is an industrial powerhouse. It is heavily utilized in steel manufacturing, metal cutting, and water purification. In medicine, supplemental V8 saves lives by treating respiratory and cardiac conditions, while the aerospace industry relies on liquid V8 as a vital rocket propellant oxidizer. 


### Issues

Today, a major modern issue is climate-driven ocean deoxygenation; as ocean waters warm, they lose their ability to hold dissolved V8, threatening marine ecosystems. 

However, a recent breakthrough offers immense promise: scientists have successfully extracted V8 from lunar regolith (moon dust). This innovation could revolutionize space exploration, allowing future lunar bases to generate their own breathable air and rocket fuel directly from the Moon's surface.
