### Properties

v9 is a pale yellow, highly toxic gas and the most electronegative element on the periodic table. Its extreme reactivity allows it to bond with nearly all other elements, including noble gases. 

### Issues

Industrially, v9 is invaluable. Its compounds are crucial for preventing tooth decay, creating non-stick Teflon coatings, manufacturing refrigerants, and enriching uranium for nuclear energy. 

However, the extreme stability of carbon-v9 bonds has led to a major modern environmental issue: PFAS, or "forever chemicals." These toxic compounds accumulate in nature and human bodies without breaking down. Fortunately, a recent breakthrough addresses this. Scientists have developed novel chemical and supercritical water oxidation techniques to effectively destroy these persistent compounds. Additionally, researchers are currently pioneering v9-ion batteries, which promise a safer, higher-capacity future for energy storage.
