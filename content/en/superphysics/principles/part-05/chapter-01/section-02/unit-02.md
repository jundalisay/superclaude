The node-nature of ideas gives them spatial properties like size and shape. 

This manifestes as big or small ideas.

We add that ideas also have shapes and angles which we call the Geometry of Ideas. 

These make ideas go together like lego blocks or go against each other like shapes that don't fit. 

These are really the 3-dimensional physicalization of wave resonance which is too abstract for 3rd-density physical minds to think of. 

Getting an idea adopted by people requires one to re-shape them to match the mold that is acceptable to those minds. 

We call this having the right approach or angles.
- A shape that has perfect angles can insert itself into a mold with less effort
- A shape with bad angles needs to be forcefully inserted just to fit the mold. Even when inside the mold, the imperfections will cause problems and might even cause the shape to be ejected back out. 
  - This is consistent with current out-of-shape ideas like Capitalism, Communism, Einstein's Relativity, Newton's Mechanics that are not totally in line with the mold of Nature. 

Superphysics explains the general shapes of Nature as the 15 layers and 2 forces so that humans can create the correctly shaped ideas that matches Nature and solves problems.
